Trust & Privacy

TriageCounsel Subprocessors

TriageCounsel uses selected third-party service providers to host the platform, deliver core functionality, and support account operations. These providers may process customer data on our behalf only as needed to provide the service. This page lists subprocessors in active use for our production environment.

Last updated: September 2026

Subprocessor Purpose Processing location Data categories (high level)
Hetzner Online GmbH Infrastructure hosting for the production application and stored customer data Germany (European Union) Account information; extracted contract text; analysis results and related customer content stored to provide the service
OpenAI, L.L.C. AI-assisted contract analysis, including plain-language explanations and, where enabled, evidence discovery or playbook-related extraction requested by the customer United States and other locations used by OpenAI for API processing (see OpenAI documentation) Contract content and analysis context as required by the features enabled for the account or action — which may range from short excerpts to broader passages or full document text depending on configuration and use
Stripe, Inc. Payment processing and subscription management United States and other locations used by Stripe (see Stripe documentation) Billing contact details and subscription metadata. Payment card details are collected directly by Stripe; TriageCounsel does not store card numbers
Google LLC Optional user authentication (Google sign-in), when enabled United States and other locations used by Google (see Google documentation) Name, email address, and authentication identifiers. No contract content
Resend, Inc. Transactional email delivery (e.g. password reset and account notifications) United States Recipient email address and content required to deliver the notification. No contract content

Original uploads

Original uploaded files (such as PDF or DOCX bytes) are processed transiently to extract text and are not retained as uploaded files. Stored contract-related content consists of extracted text and derived analysis maintained to provide review history and related features, as described in our Privacy Policy.

International transfers

Primary production hosting and storage of customer content described above is located in Germany. Some subprocessors listed above may process data outside the customer’s country, including in the United States. Where required, transfers are addressed in customer agreements and applicable data protection terms.

Changes to this list

We may update this page when subprocessors change. Customers will be notified of material subprocessor changes in accordance with the applicable data processing agreement or other contract terms.
