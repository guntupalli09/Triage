## Correction: `data_security-130` (resolved)

Direct re-check found `data_security-130`'s text ("Vendor shall have no obligation to notify Customer of any personal data breach under this Agreement.") is structured through `data_security_policy_engine.py`'s own extraction path, which does **not** call `is_operative_context` at all — it was originally attributed to the `is_operative_context` dual-signal gap in the prior mission's report, but that attribution was wrong. The actual defect, found by tracing this case precisely: `data_security`'s own dedicated `_BREACH_NOTIFICATION_NEGATION_RE` already correctly recognized the disclaimer, but the resulting `MUST_REDLINE` escalation was gated behind a specific policy threshold field being configured — an empty/under-specified policy config let a confirmed disclaimer reach a clean `ACCEPT`. Fixed by removing that gate; confirmed in the final burned-corpus replay (`FALSE_SAFE=0`, `FALSE_OPERATIVE_TO_CLEAN=0`) and via `tests/test_candidate2_data_security_time_and_negation.py::test_negation_shall_have_no_obligation`, which already expected the corrected behavior.
