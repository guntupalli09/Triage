WHEREAS, Meteor Aerospace Ltd. is engaged in the development, manufacturing and
commercialization of advanced aerospace, defense, unmanned aerial systems ("UAS"), unmanned

outstanding share capital of Meteor Aerospace Ltd.

1.1 Transaction Consideration Following Successful Flight Validation:
The Parties acknowledge and agree that the consideration payable under this Agreement shall become
due only upon (i) the successful completion of the Flight Validation, and (ii) VisionWave's
determination, acting reasonably and in good faith, that the Flight Validation has been successfully
completed in accordance with this Agreement.
Upon satisfaction of the foregoing conditions and at Closing, VisionWave shall deliver consideration
having an aggregate value of US$20,400,000, consisting of:
(a) existing issued and outstanding unrestricted common shares of VisionWave Holdings, Inc. having
an aggregate value of US$6,000,000, which shall be freely tradable upon issuance, subject only to
applicable securities laws; and
(b) newly issued restricted common shares of VisionWave Holdings, Inc. having an aggregate value
of US$14,400,000, which shall be subject to a contractual lock-up period of six (6) months from the
Closing Date. Upon expiration of such six (6) month period, such shares shall become freely
transferable, subject only to applicable U.S. securities laws, including Rule 144, if applicable.
The number of shares issued and transferred pursuant to clauses (a) and (b) shall be calculated based
upon the volume weighted average price ("WWAP") of VisionWave's common stock during the five
(5) trading days immediately preceding the Closing Date.
1.2. Purpose of the Flight Validation:
The Parties acknowledge and agree that the primary purpose of the Flight Validation is not to
demonstrate the maximum operational performance of Meteor's technologies or products, but rather to
enable VisionWave to independently verify that:
(a) Meteor Aerospace Ltd. is an active and fully operational aerospace engineering company;
(b) the engineering, technological and manufacturing capabilities represented by Meteor exist and are
operational;
(c) the Company's engineering processes, systems, technologies and development capabilities are
functioning substantially as represented to VisionWave during the negotiation process; and
(d) the Company's technical know-how and operational capabilities materially correspond to the
representations made by Meteor.
For the avoidance of doubt, the Flight Validation shall be conducted solely with respect to the Meteor
Impact 700 platform. The Flight Validation is not intended to validate, certify or reject any other
products, technologies, systems, platforms or intellectual property owned or developed by Meteor, all
of which remain included within the scope of the Transaction.

3. Aircraft subject to validation:
The aircraft subject to the Flight Validation Program shall be the Meteor Impact 700 unmanned aerial
system (the "Aircraft").
Meteor Aerospace Ltd. represents and warrants that the Aircraft is capable of achieving and sustaining
a maximum takeoff weight (MTOW) of seven hundred thirty kilograms (730 kg), an operational
endurance of up to eighteen (18) continuous flight hours, an operational ceiling of up to eighteen
thousand (18,000) feet above mean sea level, and a communications range of up to one hundred
eighty (180) kilometers under line-of-sight (LOS) operating conditions.
The Parties acknowledge that the foregoing performance specifications constitute material
representations upon which Vision Wave is relying in connection with its evaluation of the Aircraft
and its decision to enter into the Transaction. The Flight Validation Program shall include verification
of the Aircraft's ability to substantially achieve and demonstrate the foregoing capabilities under
operational testing conditions acceptable to VisionWave.
The Parties acknowledge that the foregoing performance specifications represent the Aircraft's design
objectives and maximum expected operational capabilities, and shall not be required to be
demonstrated during the Flight Validation contemplated under this Term Sheet, unless otherwise
agreed in writing by the Parties.

5. Successful Flight Validation:
For the purposes of this Agreement, the Flight Validation shall be deemed successful upon the
successful completion of a safe and controlled demonstration flight of the Meteor Impact 700.
A successful demonstration flight shall be deemed to have occurred if:
(a) the Aircraft successfully takes off under its own power;
(b) the Aircraft maintains stable and controlled flight for a continuous period of not less than five (5)
minutes;
(c) the Aircraft remains fully responsive to all flight commands throughout the demonstration;
(d) the Aircraft safely lands without any material malfunction affecting flight safety;
(e) the flight is conducted within the designated airport or approved flight test area.
For the avoidance of doubt, the Parties expressly acknowledge and agree that the successful
completion of the Flight Validation shall not require the Aircraft to demonstrate its maximum
operational altitude, maximum endurance, maximum communication range, maximum payload
capability or any other maximum operational specification represented elsewhere in this Agreement.
The purpose of the Flight Validation is solely to demonstrate the Aircraft's safe operability,
controllability and basic flight capability.
5.1 Additional Clarification Regarding Flight Validation:
The Parties further acknowledge that the Flight Validation is intended solely to verify the safe
operation, controllability, engineering integrity and fundamental flight capability of the Meteor
Impact 700 platform. It is not intended to demonstrate or verify the maximum operational
specifications, combat capabilities, commercial readiness or full performance envelope of any Meteor
products or technologies.

14. Founder retention and CTO commitment:
As a material inducement for VisionWave to enter into the Transaction, the Parties acknowledge and
agree that Mr. Yitzhak Nissan, founder of Meteor Aerospace Ltd. and former President and Chief
Executive Officer of Israel Aerospace Industries Ltd. (IAI), possesses unique technical expertise,
industry relationships, strategic know-how, and operational knowledge that are essential to the
continued growth and success of Meteor Aerospace Ltd.
Accordingly, as a condition precedent to Closing and a continuing covenant thereafter, Mr. Nissan
shall enter into a mutually acceptable executive employment and/or consulting agreement with
Meteor Aerospace Ltd. and/or VisionWave Holdings, Inc., pursuant to which he shall serve as Chief
Technology Director with specific title to be agreed, of Meteor Aerospace Ltd. for a minimum period
of three (3) years following the Closing Date.

reasonably necessary to support the strategic, technological, operational, commercial, and business
development activities of Meteor Aerospace Ltd., including without limitation:

(b) advancement of existing and future aerospace and defense technologies;

(d) assistance with technology transfer, intellectual property development, and product
commercialization;
(e) participation in strategic planning and growth initiatives approved by the Board of Directors.

15. Scope of Transaction:
The Parties acknowledge and agree that the Transaction contemplated under this Agreement includes
Meteor Aerospace Ltd. as an integrated aerospace, defense and autonomous systems company,
together with all of its existing and future products, technologies, platforms, systems, intellectual
property, know-how, software, research and development activities, manufacturing capabilities,
commercial operations and related business assets.
Without limiting the generality of the foregoing, the Transaction includes, among other things:
(a) Meteor Impact 700 — a tactical medium-altitude unmanned aerial vehicle (UAV) designed for
intelligence, surveillance, reconnaissance and multi-mission operations.
(b) Meteor Impact 1400 — a strategic MALE-class unmanned aerial platform intended for long-
endurance intelligence, surveillance and reconnaissance missions with expanded payload capabilities.
(c) Rainbow UGV — an autonomous unmanned ground vehicle (UGV) designed for logistics,
reconnaissance, force protection, remote weapon station integration and other military or homeland
security applications.
(d) Orca USV — an unmanned surface vessel (USV) designed for maritime security, coastal
protection, offshore infrastructure defense, surveillance and autonomous naval operations.
(e) Loitering Munition Systems — loitering precision-strike unmanned aerial systems capable of
surveillance, target acquisition and precision engagement.
(f) Electronic Warfare (EW) and SIGINT Systems — electronic warfare, spectrum control,
communications intelligence, signal interception, jamming and related electronic defense
technologies.
(g) C41, Command, Control, Communications, Cyber and Battle Management Systems, including
tactical communications systems, operational command-and-control software, battlefield management
platforms, sensor integration technologies and related digital defense solutions.
(h) Any additional products, technologies, prototypes, software, intellectual property, defense
systems, aerospace platforms, autonomous systems, engineering developments, research projects and
commercial offerings currently owned, developed, marketed or manufactured by Meteor Aerospace
Ltd., whether or not specifically identified in this Agreement.
For the avoidance of doubt, the Flight Validation requirements under this Agreement apply solely to
the Meteor Impact 700 platform for purposes of satisfying the applicable condition precedent to
Closing. The successful validation of the Meteor Impact 700 shall not limit, exclude or otherwise

16. Strategic Use of the Transaction Consideration:
The Parties expressly acknowledge and agree that the aggregate consideration having a value of
US$20,400,000 payable pursuant to this Agreement constitutes the agreed purchase price for
Vision Wave's acquisition of fifty-one percent (51%) of the issued and outstanding share capital of
Meteor Aerospace Ltd.
The Parties further acknowledge and agree that the economic purpose and intent of the Transaction is
to strengthen and expand the business of Meteor Aerospace Ltd. Accordingly, the Parties intend that
the full value of the Transaction consideration shall be utilized for the continued growth and
development of Meteor Aerospace Ltd., including, without limitation:
(a) research and development;
(b) engineering and product development;
(c) manufacturing and production capabilities;
(d) marketing and sales activities;
(e) recruitment, retention and compensation of employees and key personnel;
(f) business development and commercial expansion;
(g) working capital and general corporate purposes; and
(h) any other investments reasonably required to support the long-term growth and operations of
Meteor Aerospace Ltd.
