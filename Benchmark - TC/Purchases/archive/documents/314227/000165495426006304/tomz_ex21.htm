AGREEMENT AND PLAN OF MERGER

 

THIS AGREEMENT AND PLAN OF MERGER (this “Agreement”) is made and entered into as of June 28, 2026, by and among TOMI ENVIRONMENTAL SOLUTIONS, INC., a Florida corporation (“TOMZ”), TOMZ MERGER SUB, INC., a Delaware corporation and wholly owned subsidiary of TOMZ (“Merger Sub”), and CARBONIUM CORE, INC., a Delaware corporation (the “Company”). Certain capitalized terms used in this Agreement are defined in Section 1.

 

RECITALS

 

A. TOMZ and the Company intend to effect a merger of Merger Sub with and into the Company (the “Merger”) in accordance with this Agreement and the DGCL. Upon consummation of the Merger, Merger Sub will cease to exist, and the Company will become a wholly owned subsidiary of TOMZ.

 

B. The Parties intend that the Merger will qualify as a “reorganization” within the meaning of Section 368(a) of the Code and the Treasury Regulations, and that this Agreement be, and hereby is, adopted as a “plan of reorganization” for the purposes of Section 368 of the Code and Treasury Regulations Sections 1.368-2(g) and 1.368-3 (the “Intended Tax Treatment”).

 

C. The TOMZ Board has (i) determined that the Contemplated Transactions are fair to, advisable and in the best interests of TOMZ and its shareholders, (ii) approved and declared advisable this Agreement, the Transaction Documents and the Contemplated Transactions, including the issuance of shares of TOMZ Common Stock to the stakeholders of the Company pursuant to the terms of this Agreement and (iii) determined to recommend, if deemed necessary by TOMZ, upon the terms and subject to the conditions set forth in this Agreement, that the shareholders of TOMZ vote to approve this Agreement and thereby approve the Contemplated Transactions and against any competing proposals, including the issuance of shares of TOMZ Common Stock to the equity holders of the Company pursuant to the terms of this Agreement and, if deemed necessary by TOMZ, an amendment to TOMZ’s articles of incorporation to effect the Nasdaq Reverse Split.

 

D. The Merger Sub Board has (i) determined that the Contemplated Transactions are fair to, advisable, and in the best interests of Merger Sub and its sole stockholder, (ii) approved and declared advisable this Agreement, the Transaction Documents and the Contemplated Transactions and (iii) determined to recommend, upon the terms and subject to the conditions set forth in this Agreement, that the stockholder of Merger Sub votes to adopt this Agreement and thereby approve the Contemplated Transactions.

 

E. The Company Board has (i) determined that the Contemplated Transactions are fair to, advisable and in the best interests of the Company and its equity holders, (ii) approved and declared advisable this Agreement, the Transaction Documents and the Contemplated Transactions and (iii) determined to recommend, upon the terms and subject to the conditions set forth in this Agreement, that the equity holders of the Company (a) vote to adopt this Agreement, the Transaction Documents and thereby approve the Contemplated Transactions, and (b) consent to the conversion of the Series C Preferred Stock issued pursuant to this Agreement into shares of TOMZ Common Stock (the “Conversion”), and (iv) authorized the TOMZ Board to change TOMZ’s name to “Carbonium Core, Inc.” subsequent to the Conversion (the “Name Change”).

 

F. Concurrently with the execution and delivery of this Agreement, the Company, TOMZ, and Affiliates of TOMZ are entering into a Support Agreement, (the “TOMZ Support Agreement”), providing that, among other things, such Affiliates will vote their shares of TOMZ Capital Stock in favor of this Agreement and the Contemplated Transactions (including the Merger).
