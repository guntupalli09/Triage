MEMBER INTEREST AND ASSET EXCHANGE AGREEMENT
This Member Interest and Asset Exchange Agreement (this "Agreement") is made and entered into
as of April 1, 2026 (the “Agreement Date”), by and among Transportation and Logistics Systems,
Inc., a Nevada corporation ("TLSS"), TLSS Acquisition, Inc., a Delaware corporation and a
wholly-owned subsidiary of TLSS ("TA"), TLSS Reverse PGS, LLC, a Texas limited liability
company, a wholly-owned subsidiary of TA (“Reverse”), Badcer Ops, Inc., a Nevada corporation
("Seller"), Jeff Badders (“Badders”) and Mercer Street Global Opportunity Fund, LLC, a Delaware
limited liability company (“Mercer”), more particularly identified on Schedule 1 attached hereto
and made a part hereof (the “Seller Shareholders”), Patriot Glass Solutions, LLC, a Texas limited
liability company (“PGS”) and Michael Wanke (“Wanke”), the twenty (20%) percent owner and
the sole Manager of PGS, of which the Seller is an eighty (80%) percent owner. Each of the parties
to this Agreement is individually referred to herein as a “Party” and collectively, as the “Parties.”
Certain capitalized terms are defined in Article X.
RECITALS
A. The Seller owns eighty (80%) percent of the member interests of PGS (the “Interests”), as
well as four (4) “Patents” (as defined in Section 1.6 hereof), representing substantially all of its
assets, and, subject to the terms and conditions set forth herein, the Seller desires to exchange with
TA (i) one hundred (100%) percent of the Seller’s Interests and (11) the Patents for the TLSS Series
J Preferred Shares (as defined below) of TLSS.
B. TA desires to combine Reverse, its wholly-owned subsidiary, with the business of PGS,
through a reverse triangular merger of Reverse with and into PGS, with PGS as the surviving entity
(the "Merger"), pursuant to which (i) all of Seller’s Interests of PGS owned by Seller at the
“Effective Time” (as defined in Section 1.2), and (ii) the Patents, will be exchanged, transferred
and assigned to TA, all in exchange for the Seller receiving, in the aggregate, Forty Seven
Thousand Five Hundred (47,500) shares of “TLSS Series J Preferred Shares” (as defined in Section
1.7(b)).
C. Upon “Closing” (as defined herein), PGS shall be eighty (80%) percent owned by TA and
twenty (20%) percent owned by Wanke, and TA shall grant one or more licenses to PGS to enable
PGS to utilize the Patents being acquired by TA from the Seller.
D. The Board of Directors of Seller has determined that the Merger is consistent with and in
furtherance of the long-term business strategy of Seller and Seller desires to combine the business
of PGS with certain businesses operated by TLSS and TA directly and through its subsidiaries,
including following the Merger, PGS, and for the Seller Shareholders to have a continuing equity
interest in the combined businesses through the Seller Shareholders’ ownership of 47,500 TLSS
Series J Preferred Shares following the liquidation of Seller;
E. The holders of the TLSS Series J Preferred Shares shall have the right to vote with the
shares of “TLSS Common Stock” (as defined in Section 2.7 hereof), on an as-converted to
common stock basis, with respect to all matters on which the holders of TLSS Common Stock are
entitled to vote, subject to any applicable beneficial ownership limitations.
