UNWIND, TERMINATION AND SHARE EXCHANGE AGREEMENT
This UNWIND, TERMINATION AND SHARE EXCHANGE AGREEMENT
(the “Agreement”), is made and entered into this seventeenth (17) day of February 2026,
by and among PROTAGENIC THERAPEUTICS, INC., a Delaware corporation (“PTIX”),
PHYTANIX BIO, a Nevada corporation (“Phytanix”), and ALTEROLA BIOTECH INC.,
a Nevada corporation (“Alterola”), EMC2 Capital LLC, a Wyoming limited liability
company (“EMC2”), each of the persons listed on Schedule A hereto (collectively with
Alterola and EMC2, the “Former Phytanix Stockholders”) and COLIN STOTT, an individual,
as the Sellers’ Representative pursuant to the terms of the SEA (as defined below). Capitalized
terms used in this Agreement but not otherwise defined shall have the meanings ascribed to
such terms in the SEA.
RECITALS
WHEREAS, on or about May 15, 2025 (the “Phytanix Closing Date”), PTIX , each
of the Former Phytanix Stockholders and the Sellers’ Representative entered into, and
consummated the transactions contemplated by, that certain Share Exchange Agreement
dated as of May 15, 2025 (the “SEA”) whereby, among other things, PTIX acquired all of
the issued and outstanding Shares and Preferred Shares, representing 100% of the issued
and outstanding capital Phytanix, from the Former Phytanix Stockholders in exchange for
the Closing Consideration.
WHEREAS, following the Phytanix Closing Date, certain disputes have arisen
between PTIX and the Former Phytanix Stockholders in connection with the SEA, and
PTIX commenced a litigation against the Former Phytanix Stockholders, in The Court of
Chancery of the State of Delaware, in an action entitled Protagenic Therapeutics, Inc. v.
Alterola Biotech Inc., et al., bearing Case No. 2025-1238-KMM (the “Litigation”); and
WHEREAS, in settlement of the Litigation, PTIX and the Former Phytanix
Stockholders have entered into that certain Settlement Agreement dated as of February 13,
2026 which calls for the parties to execute and deliver this Agreement for the purpose of
terminating the SEA and unwinding the transactions contemplated by the SEA, including,
without limitation, the Share Purchase.
NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the foregoing premises and
representations, warranties, covenants and agreements contained herein, and for other good
and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged,
and intending to be legally bound hereby, the parties hereto hereby agree as follows:
