ASSET PURCHASE AGREEMENT
This ASSET PURCHASE AGREEMENT (the “Agreement”) is made and entered into as
of November 20, 2025, by and between MICRO IMAGING TECHNOLOGY INC., a California
corporation (“Buyer”), and LESLIE T. GLADSTONE (“Seller”), solely in her role as Assignee
for the Benefit of Creditors of Med-Acsthetic Solutions, Inc. dba SaltMED (“SaltMED”). Each
of Seller and Buyer is a “Party” and collectively they are the “Parties” to this Agreement.
WITNESSETH:
WHEREAS, on August 26, 2025 (“Assignment Date”), Salt MED, as assignor, and Seller,
as assignee, entered into a certain General Assignment for the Benefit of Creditors (Corporation),
by which SaltMED assigned to Seller all the assets of SaltMED for the benefit of SaltMED’s
creditors.
WHEREAS, Seller currently operates SaltMED’s business of selling the SaltFacial, a
Class I, FDA-cleared medical device, and related supplies.
WHEREAS, on the terms and subject to the conditions set forth in this Agreement, Seller
desires to sell, transfer, and assign to Buyer, and Buyer desires to purchase, acquire, and assume
from Seller, all the Assets (described below) free and clear of all Interests, Liens and Claims, all
as more specifically provided herein.
WHEREAS, Buyer understands that this Agreement may be one of multiple agreements
being provided by Seller to various potential purchasers.
NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the premises and the mutual covenants and
agreements hereinafter contained, the Parties hereby agree as follows:
