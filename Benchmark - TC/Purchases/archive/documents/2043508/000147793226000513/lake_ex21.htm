AGREEMENT AND PLAN OF MERGER

 

AND BUSINESS COMBINATION AGREEMENT

 

This AGREEMENT AND PLAN OF MERGER AND BUSINESS COMBINATION AGREEMENT (the “Agreement”), dated as of January 23, 2026 (the “Signing Date”), by and among Lake Superior Acquisition Corp., a British Virgin Islands business company (“Predecessor”), Openmarkets Group Pty Ltd, an Australian proprietary limited company Australian Company Number 660 155 000 (the “Company”), BMYG OMG Pty Ltd, an Australian proprietary limited company Australian Company Number 620 123 471, as trustee for the BMYG OMG Unit Trust (the “Shareholder”), as well as such other persons who are contemplated to later join this Agreement as the “Purchaser” and “Merger Sub” herein. The Predecessor, Purchaser, Merger Sub, Company, and Shareholder are sometimes referred to herein individually as a “Party” and, collectively, as the “Parties.”

 

RECITALS:

 

A. The Company and its Subsidiaries (the “Company Group”) are an Australian B2B financial services, technology and trading infrastructure provider headquartered in Sydney, with additional offices in Melbourne and Brisbane, Australia. The Company Group’s main offerings include brokerage services and wealth management technology solutions, and primarily serve three client groups: (i) financial technology providers, (ii) advice professionals, including private wealth advisers and financial advisers, dealer groups and stockbrokers, and (iii) professional (high-volume) traders (the “Business”).

 

B. Predecessor is a blank check company formed for the sole purpose of entering into a share exchange, asset acquisition, share purchase, recapitalization, reorganization or other similar business combination with one or more businesses or entities.

 

C. Purchaser is to be a public limited company incorporated under the Corporations Act 2001 (Cth) in Australia.

 

D. Merger Sub is to be a British Virgin Islands business company incorporated as a wholly-owned subsidiary of Purchaser for the purpose of consummating the Transactions (as defined below).

 

E. The Parties desire to merge Merger Sub with and into Predecessor with Predecessor continuing as the surviving corporation (the “Initial Merger”), so that following the Initial Merger, (i) Predecessor becomes a wholly-owned subsidiary of Purchaser, (ii) the securityholders of Predecessor receive securities in Purchaser in exchange for their securities in Predecessor, and (iii) Predecessor will, by operation of law, acceded to all of the contracts, acquire all of the assets and assume all of the liabilities of Merger Sub.

 

F. In connection with and immediately after the Initial Merger, Purchaser desires to liquidate Predecessor and have Predecessor transfer all of its assets to Purchaser.

 

G. In connection with and immediately, or within a reasonable amount of time not to exceed two days, after the Initial Merger, Purchaser wishes to acquire the Company by issuing Purchaser Shares to the Shareholder in exchange for the transfer by the Shareholder of all of the Company Shares owned by the Shareholder (the “Acquisition Transfer and Exchange”).

H. For U.S. federal income tax purposes, the Parties hereto intend that the Initial Merger and Acquisition Transfer and Exchange will qualify as a “transfer to a corporation controlled by the transferor” within the meaning of Section 351 of the Code, or that the Initial Merger and Acquisition Contribution and Exchange will separately qualify as “reorganizations” within the meaning of Section 368(a) of the Code.

 

I. The Boards of Directors of the Company and the Shareholder have determined that this Agreement, the Transfer and Exchange and the other transactions contemplated by this Agreement and the Additional Documents (collectively, the “Transactions”) are fair and advisable to, and in the best interests of, the Company and the Shareholder.

 

J. The Board of Directors of Predecessor has determined that this Agreement, the Transfer and Exchange, and the other Transactions are fair and advisable to, and in the best interests of Predecessor and its stockholders.
