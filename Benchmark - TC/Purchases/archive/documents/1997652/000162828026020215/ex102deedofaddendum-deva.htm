Background
A. The parties entered into the Asset Sale Agreement under which the Seller agreed to sell the
Assets to the Buyer, which included the Dev A++ Assets.
The JVSA sets out, inter alia, the Checkerboard Strategy that has been agreed by the parties.
At the time of entering into the Asset Sale Agreement, DWE and the Seller intended to apply
for a Retention Licence over the Dev A++ Area. Under the terms of the JVSA Deed of
Addendum, DWE and the Seller have now agreed that a Retention Licence will be applied for
over the P2DA, which incorporates the Dev A++ Area.
The parties have entered into this Deed to amend the Asset Sale Agreement to reflect that the
Buyer has agreed that it will only acquire a beneficial interest in the Dev A++ Area and there
will not be a Retention Licence applied for over the Dev A++ Area. This is intended to facilitate
the relevant parties being able to apply for and hold the P2DA RL under and in accordance with
the JVSA Deed of Addendum.
