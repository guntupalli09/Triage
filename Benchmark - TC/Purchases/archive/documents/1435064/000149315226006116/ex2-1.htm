Recitals

WHEREAS, Seller wishes to sell and assign to Buyer, and Owner desires to cause Seller to sell and assign to Buyer, and Buyer wishes to purchase and assume from Seller, substantially all the assets, and certain specified liabilities, of the Business, subject to the terms and conditions set forth herein; and
