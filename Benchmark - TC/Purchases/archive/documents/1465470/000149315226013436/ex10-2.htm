Intellectual Property Assignment Agreement
This intellectual property assignment agreement (“Agreement”) is being entered into between
Hydrenesis, Inc (“Assignor) and Natural Shrimp, Incorporated (“Assignee”) effective this 17%
day of March 2026
Whereas, the parties have entered into that certain Intellectual Property Acquisition and
Transition Management Agreement dated March 17", 2026 (the “Master Agreement”); and
Pursuant to the Master Agreement, Assignor makes the following assignment to Assignee.
Now therefore, the parties agree as follows:
Definitions
“Assigned Intellectual Property” means only and exclusively the intellectual property assets
identified on Schedule A attached hereto, including, without limitation:
(a) all issued patents, patent applications, provisional applications, continuations, continuations-
in-part, divisionals, reissues, reexaminations, and foreign counterparts;
(b) all inventions, discoveries, improvements, and developments embodied therein;
(c) all trade secrets, technical know-how, and proprietary processes (described by category, not
content);
(d) all engineering drawings, designs, schematics, specifications, and manufacturing processes;
(e) all test data, research data, validation data, and technical documentation;
(f) all software, source code, object code, algorithms, and related documentation, if any;
(g) all trademarks, service marks, trade names, and associated goodwill, whether registered or
unregistered; and
(h) all domain names and related digital assets, if any.
1. ASSIGNMENT; TIMING; NO IMPLIED RIGHTS
Assignor hereby transfers the Assigned Intellectual Property to Assignee for the consideration set
forth in the Master Agreement.
2. FURTHER ASSURANCES
As required in furtherance of this Agreement, Hydrenesis shall, at the Company’s reasonable
expense, execute and deliver such further instruments and take such further actions as may be
reasonably necessary to perfect, record, or enforce the Company’s ownership of the Assigned
Intellectual Property, including recordation with the United States Patent and Trademark Office
and applicable foreign authorities.

 

 
 

 

3. GOVERNING LAW
This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws specified in the
Master Agreement
IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the Parties have executed this Agreement as of the date first written
above.
