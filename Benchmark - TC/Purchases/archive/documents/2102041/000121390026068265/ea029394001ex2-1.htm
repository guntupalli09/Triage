BUSINESS COMBINATION AGREEMENT

 

This Business Combination Agreement (this “Agreement”) is made and entered into as of June 8, 2026 by and among Inflection Point Acquisition Corp. VI, a Cayman Islands exempted company (which shall transfer by way of continuation to and domesticate as a Delaware corporation prior to the Closing) (the “Purchaser”), IPFX PubCo, Inc., a Delaware corporation and wholly owned subsidiary of the Purchaser (“Pubco”), IPFX Merger Sub, Inc., a Delaware corporation and direct, wholly owned subsidiary of Pubco (“Merger Sub”), and Quantum Space, LLC, a Delaware limited liability company (the “Company”). The Purchaser, Pubco, Merger Sub and the Company are sometimes referred to herein individually as a “Party” and, collectively, as the “Parties.”
