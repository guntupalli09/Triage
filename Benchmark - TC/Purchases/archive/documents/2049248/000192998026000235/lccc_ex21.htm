MERGER AGREEMENT

 

This MERGER AGREEMENT (the “Agreement”), dated as of May 22, 2026 (the “Signing Date”), by and among CPRO Electronics Holding Limited, a British Virgin Islands business company (the “Company”), CPRO Electronics Co., Ltd., a South Korean company (“CPRO Korea”) (solely with respect to Section 14.18 hereof), Lakeshore Acquisition III Corp., a Cayman Islands exempted company (the “Parent”), CPRO Holding Limited, a Cayman Islands exempted company (the “Purchaser”) and LCCC Merger Sub Inc., a British Virgin Islands business company and wholly owned Subsidiary of the Parent (the “Merger Sub”).

 

WITNESSETH:

 

A. The Company, through its wholly owned and Controlled (as defined below) Subsidiaries (as defined below), is a manufacturer of optical products intended for the teleconference, surveillance, and security industry (the “Business”);

 

B. Prior to the Closing, the Company shall acquire 100% of the issued share capital of CPRO Korea, which in turn owns all of the equity interest of (i) CPRO Vietnam Company Limited, a Vietnamese company (“CPRO Vietnam”) and (ii) CPRO Americas Inc, a Nevada corporation (“CPRO America”) (the “Reorganization”);

 

C. The Parent is a blank check company formed for the sole purpose of entering into a merger, share exchange, asset acquisition, share purchase, recapitalization, reorganization or similar business combination with one or more businesses or entities;

 

D. The Purchaser is a Cayman Islands exempted company and a wholly owned Subsidiary of the Parent, formed for the sole purpose of the merger of the Parent with and into the Purchaser, in which the Purchaser will be the surviving company (the “Reincorporation Merger”), upon the terms and subject to the conditions set forth herein and in accordance with the applicable provisions of the Companies Act (as revised) of the Cayman Islands (the “Cayman Companies Act”);

 

E. The parties hereto desire that the Merger Sub, a wholly owned subsidiary of Parent formed for the sole purpose of merging with and into the Company, and which shall become a wholly owned subsidiary of the Purchaser as a consequence of the Reincorporation Merger, be merged with and into the Company at least one Business Day after the completion of the Reincorporation Merger, such that the Company will be the surviving company (the “Acquisition Merger”), upon the terms and subject to the conditions set forth herein and in accordance with the applicable provisions of the BVI Business Companies Act (as revised) (the “BVI Companies Act”); and

 

F. For United States federal income tax purposes, the parties intend that the Reincorporation Merger will qualify for the Reincorporation Intended Tax Treatment (as defined in Section 2.9), that the Acquisition Merger will qualify for the Acquisition Intended Tax Treatment (as defined in Section 3.8), and that this Agreement be, and hereby is, adopted as a “plan of reorganization” for each of the Reincorporation Merger and the Acquisition Merger for purposes of Section 368 of the Code and Treasury Regulations Section 1.368-2(g) and 1.368-3(a).

 

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the premises set forth above, which are incorporated in this Agreement as if fully set forth below, and the representations, warranties, covenants and agreements contained in this Agreement, and intending to be legally bound hereby, the parties accordingly agree as follows:
