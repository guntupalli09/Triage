BACKGROUND
WHEREAS, Seller is in the business of researching, developing, manufacturing and commercializing pharmaceutical products, including the pharmaceutical product internally known as KZR-261 and certain discovery compounds targeting the Sec61 translocon or protein secretion pathway;
