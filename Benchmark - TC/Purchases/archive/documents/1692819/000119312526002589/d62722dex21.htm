PURCHASE AND SALE AGREEMENT

This Purchase and Sale Agreement is entered into as of December 31, 2025, by and between Q-Generation Holdings, LLC, a Delaware limited liability company (“Seller”), Vistra Operations Company LLC, a Delaware limited liability company (“Buyer”), and solely for the limited purposes set forth in Section 2.2(b), Section 2.3(c)(iii), Section 6.15, Section 6.16, Section 6.22, Section 8.1 and Article XI (the “Buyer Parent Provisions”), Vistra Corp., a Delaware corporation (“Buyer Parent”). Each of Seller, Buyer and, with respect to those references contained in the Buyer Parent Provisions, Buyer Parent is, individually, a “Party,” and, collectively, they are the “Parties.”

W I T N E S E T H:

WHEREAS, Seller owns all of the issued and outstanding Interests of Q-Generation, LLC, a Delaware limited liability company (the “Company” and such Interests, the “Company Interests”);

WHEREAS, the Company owns all of the issued and outstanding Interests of Cogentrix Master HoldCo I, LLC, a Delaware limited liability company (“Master HoldCo”);

WHEREAS, Master HoldCo owns all of the issued and outstanding Interests of (a) Cogentrix Power Management LLC, a Delaware limited liability company (“CPM”), and (b) Cogentrix Finance HoldCo I, LLC, a Delaware limited liability company (“CFH”);

WHEREAS, CPM and CFH collectively own, directly and indirectly, all of the Acquired Companies (other than the Company, Master Holdco, CPM and CFH);

WHEREAS, at the Closing, Seller desires to sell to Buyer, and Buyer desires to purchase from Seller, the Company Interests, on the terms and subject to the conditions set forth herein; and

WHEREAS, concurrently with the execution and delivery of this Agreement, Seller, Buyer and the other parties thereto have entered into the Hamilton Purchase Agreement, pursuant to which Merger Sub will merge with and into Hamilton with Hamilton surviving the merger as the surviving company and continuing as an indirect wholly owned subsidiary of the Company.
