TERMINATION AGREEMENT
This TERMINATION AGREEMENT (this “Agreement”) is entered into as of January 15, 2026
(the “Effective Date”), by and among KM QUAD, a Cayman Islands exempted company (the
“Company”), Quetta Acquisition Corporation, a Delaware corporation (the “Parent”), Quad
Global Inc., a Cayman Islands exempted company (the “Purchaser”), and Quad Group Inc., a
Cayman Islands exempted company (the “Merger Sub”). The Company, Parent, Purchaser, and
Merger Sub are referred to collectively as the “Parties,”
RECITALS
WHEREAS, the Parties entered into that certain Agreement and Plan of Merger, dated as of
February 14, 2025 (the “Merger Agreement”);
WHEREAS, consummation of the transactions contemplated by the Merger Agreement was
subject to, among other things, applicable approvals and/or filings with the China Securities
Regulatory Commission (“CSRC”);
WHEREAS, due to the prolonged CSRC approval process, after mutual discussion and
assessment by the parties, the parties have determined ‘not to proceed with the transactions
contemplated by the Merger Agreement; and
WHEREAS, the Parties desire to terminate the Merger Agreement by mutual written agreement
and to set forth their respective rights and obli gations in connection therewith.
NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements herein, the
Parties agree as follows:
1. TERMINATION OF MERGER AGREEMENT
Pursuant to Article XIII of the Merger Agreement and by mutual agreement of the Parties, the
Merger Agreement is hereby terminated in its entirety, effective as of the Effective Date. Except
as expressly provided in this Agreement, the Merger Agreement shall be null and void and of no
further force or effect as of the Effective Date.
2. TERMINATION PAYMENT

(a) Payment Obligation. In consideration of the termination of the Merger Agreement, the
Company shall pay to the Parent an aggregate amount of $170,900 (the “Termination Payment”).
(b) Composition of Termination Payment. The Termination Payment consists of (i) $110,900 in
reimbursement of out-of-pocket transaction expenses, including $50,000 for printer fees, $60,000
for the fairness opinion, and $900 for CUSIP fees, plus (ii) an additional $60,000 representing the
January extension payment.
(c) Timing and Method. The Termination Payment shall be paid concurrently with the execution
of this Agreement by wire transfer of immediately available funds to an account designated in
writing by the Parent.
(d) No Setoff. The Termination Payment shall be paid without setoff, counterclaim, or deduction.
(€) The termination Payment shall constitute full and final settlement of any and all obligations
relating to the Merger Agreement.
3. MUTUAL RELEASE
Effective upon receipt by the Parent of the Termination Payment, each Party, on behalf of itself
and its respective affiliates, directors, officers, shareholders, employees, and representatives,
hereby releases and forever discharges the other Parties and their respective affiliates from any
and all claims, all notes, demands, liabilities, or causes of action, whether known or unknown,
arising out of or relating to the Merger Agreement or the transactions contemplated thereby to the
fullest extent permitted by applicable law, except for claims arising under this Agreement.
4. NO ADMISSION OF LIABILITY
The termination of the Merger Agreement and the execution of this Agreement shall not
constitute an admission of fault or liability by any Party, all of which are expressly denied.
5. CONFIDENTIALITY; PUBLIC DISCLOSURE
Notwithstanding anything to the contrary herein, the Parent may disclose the existence and
material terms of this Agreement in any SEC filing or public disclosure as required by applicable
law or stock exchange rules.
6. GOVERNING LAW

This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of
New York, without regard to conflict of laws principles.
7, ENTIRE AGREEMENT
This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement among the Parties with respect to the subject
matter hereof and supersedes all prior discussions or understandings relating thereto.
8. COUNTERPARTS; ELECTRONIC SIGNATURES
This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, including by PDF or electronic signature, each
of which shall be deemed an original.
