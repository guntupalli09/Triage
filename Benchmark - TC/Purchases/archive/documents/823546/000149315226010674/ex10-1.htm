PROPOSED NON-BINDING TERM SHEET
(Asset Purchase Transaction — RocketFuel Blockchain, Inc. / RPay Inc. & RPoints, Inc.)
Date: 13 = March 2026
This term sheet (this “Term Sheet”) summarizes the principal terms and conditions of a proposed
asset sale by RocketFuel Blockchain. Inc.. a Nevada corporation (“Seller”). to RPay, Inc.. a
Delaware corporation. and RPoints. Inc.. a Delaware corporation (collectively. the “Buyers”).
This Term Sheet is for discussion purposes only and does not constitute a binding agreement or
commitment to consununate the transaction, except for the sections expressly identified below as
Binding Provisions (Break-Up Fee, Exclusivity, Confidentiality, Fees and Expenses. Public
Disclosure Discipline, and Governing Law) (collectively, the “Binding Provisions”). No other
legally binding obligation will arise unless and until definitive agreements are executed and
delivered by the parties.
1. Parties
1.1. Seller: RocketFuel Blockchain, Inc., a publicly traded Nevada corporation.
1.2. Buyers: RPay, Inc. and RPoints, Inc., each a Delaware corporation.
1.3. Seller and Buyer may be referred to individually as a “Party” and collectively as the
“Parties.”
2. Non-Binding Nature; Definitive Documentation
2.1. Except for the Binding Provisions, this Term Sheet is non-binding. The transaction will
be documented in a definitive Asset Purchase Agreement and related closing documents
(collectively, the “Definitive Agreements’’) in form and substance reasonably acceptable
to the Parties and their counsel.
3. Transaction Overview & Structure
3.1. Asset Acquisition. At the Closing (as defined below). Buyer shall purchase from Seller,
and Seller shall transfer. assign. sell and deliver to Buyer, free and clear of any Liens (other
than Permitted Liens). all of Seller's right, title and interest in the assets used primarily in
the RKFL Payments Business and the RKFL Loyalty Business (collectively. the
“Purchased Assets”).
3.2.Entity Allocation. RPay, Inc. shall acquire the RKFL Payments Business. RPoints, Inc.
shall acquire the RKFL Loyalty Business.

The Purchased Assets include the assets used primarily in Seller's operating product lines,

5. Assumed vs. Excluded Liabilities
The Buyers will assume only those liabilities expressly identified in the definitive agreements.
consisting solely of the specified deferred compensation obligations (allocated between the
Buyers) and post-Closing performance obligations under assigned contracts. Seller retains all other
legacy liabilities, including SEC compliance matters, taxes, and trade payables.
6. Consideration and Structure (Aggregate)
Total Deal Value (approx.): $5.700,000.00. comprised of:
6.1. Debt Assumption / Deferred Compensation (Cap). The Buyers will assume specified
deferred compensation obligations of Seller owed to Peter M. Jensen and Bennett J.
Yankowitz, capped at $1.500.000.00 in aggregate principal. (To be allocated in the APAs:
$1,000,000) to RPay. Inc.: $500,000 to RPoints, Inc.).
6.2. Earn-Out (Cap). RPay., Inc. shall pay Seller 20% of Net Revenue generated by the RKFL
Business, paid quarterly, until the earlier of: (a) two (2) years after Closing. or (b)
$2.500.000.00 has been paid in the aggregate.
6.3. Equity Warrant (Value). At Closing, Seller shall receive Warrants to acquire 20% of
the fully diluted equity of RPay, Inc., and 20% of the fully diluted equity of RPoints, Inc.
The Warrants shall be exercisable two (2) years from Closing. The Warrants shall be
subject to a Buyer repurchase/call right priced at a fixed $1,500,000.00 for the RPay, Inc.
Warrant, and $200,000.00 for the RPoints, Inc. Warrant, and otherwise on terms set forth
in the APAs.

BINDING PROVISIONS
12.Exclusivity and Break-Up Fee (Binding)
i274. Exclusivity. For 90 days following execution of this Term Sheet (the “Exclusivity
Period”), Seller shall not (and shall cause its representatives not to) solicit, initiate,
knowingly encourage, or enter into discussions regarding any competing sale of the
Purchased Assets or a similar transaction involving the RKFL Payments Business or
RKFL Loyalty Business.
122. Break-Up Fee. Seller shall pay Buyers a break-up fee if Seller: (a) terminates
exclusivity during the Exclusivity Period other than for Buyer's material breach: (b)
abandons the transaction or refuses to negotiate and execute the Definitive Agreements in
good faith on substantially the terms set forth herein: or (c) enters into or consummates a
competing transaction involving the Purchased Assets during the Exclusivity Period or
within nine (9) months after its expiration.
The break-up fee shall equal $100,000.00 for each month (or portion thereof) that has
commenced during the period from the date of execution of this Term Sheet through the
termination/competing transaction date. This fee shall be deemed to accrue in full at the
start of each monthly period and shall not be pro-rated for any partial month.
13.Confidentiality (Binding)
The existence and terms of this Term Sheet and all non-public information exchanged will be kept
confidential, except as required by law, SEC disclosure obligations, or to advisors under
confidentiality obligations.
14.Fees and Expenses (Binding)
Each Party bears its own legal, accounting, and advisory costs unless otherwise agreed in writing.
15.Public Disclosure Discipline (Binding)
Neither Party will issue a press release or public announcement regarding the transaction without
the other Party’s prior written consent, except as required by law or SEC rules (in which case the
disclosing Party will provide reasonable advance notice and a chance to review, to the extent
permitted).
16.Governing Law; Venue (Binding)
This Term Sheet and the Binding Provisions will be governed by the laws of the State of Nevada.
without regard to conflicts-of-law principles. Exclusive venue shall be state or federal courts
located in Clark County. Nevada.
