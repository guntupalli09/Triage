AMENDED AND RESTATED ARRANGEMENT AGREEMENT

THIS AMENDED AND RESTATED ARRANGEMENT AGREEMENT is made as of February 23, 2026,

BETWEEN:

Aptose Biosciences Inc., a corporation existing under the laws of Canada (the “Corporation”)

- and -

HS North America Ltd., a corporation incorporated under the laws of Alberta (the “Purchaser”)

- and

Hanmi Pharmaceutical Co. Ltd., a corporation existing under the laws of the Republic of Korea (“Parent”)

WHEREAS the Corporation, the Purchaser and Parent entered into an arrangement agreement dated November 18, 2025 (the “Original Agreement”), involving, among other things, the acquisition by the Purchaser of all the issued and outstanding Shares (other than the Excluded Shares) in accordance with the terms set out herein;

AND WHEREAS the Corporation, the Purchaser and Parent wish to carry out the transactions contemplated by this Agreement by way of a plan of arrangement of Aptose under Section 193 of the ABCA;

AND WHEREAS the Board unanimously determined that it would be in the best interests of the Corporation to enter into this Agreement and to complete the transactions contemplated herein;

AND WHEREAS the Corporation, the Purchaser and Parent now desire, in accordance with Section 8.1 of the Original Agreement, to amend and restate the Original Agreement to reflect certain changes determined necessary and in the best interest of each of the Corporation, the Purchaser and Parent;

NOW THEREFORE in consideration of the covenants and agreements herein contained, the Parties agree as follows:
