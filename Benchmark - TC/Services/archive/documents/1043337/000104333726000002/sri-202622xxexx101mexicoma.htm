MEXICO MANUFACTURING AGREEMENT

This Mexico Manufacturing Agreement (the “Agreement”), dated as of January 30, 2026 (the “Effective Date”), is entered into by and between Stoneridge Electronics, Inc. (the “Supplier”) and Stoneridge Control Devices, Inc. (“Buyer”). Supplier and Buyer are each a “Party” and are collectively referred to as the “Parties.”

WHEREAS, Control Devices Acquisition, LLC (“Parent”), Supplier and certain Affiliates (as defined below) of Supplier entered into that certain Stock Purchase Agreement, dated as of January 30, 2026 (the “Purchase Agreement”), pursuant to which Parent purchased certain equity interests from Supplier and such Affiliates, including 100% of the equity interests of Buyer;

WHEREAS, as part of the transactions contemplated under the Purchase Agreement, Supplier agrees to manufacture (itself or through its Affiliates) and supply to customers of Buyer certain Products (as defined below) at a production facility owned and operated by Ted de Mexico, S. de R.L. de C.V., an Affiliate of Supplier that is located in Ciudad Juarez, Chihuahua, Mexico (the “Maquiladora”), using machinery, equipment, tools and other assets acquired by Buyer under the Purchase Agreement; and
WHEREAS, the Supplier and the Maquiladora have entered into that certain Maquila Services Agreement, dated January 1, 2025 (the “Maquiladora Agreement”), pursuant to which the Maquiladora will manufacture for Supplier certain Products (as defined below) as well as other Supplier products not related to this Agreement.
NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements hereinafter set forth, the Parties hereto agree as follows:
Section 1.    Supply of Products.
1.1    Purchase and Sale.
(a)    Subject to the terms and conditions of this Agreement, during the Term (as defined below), (i) Supplier shall continue to intake customer orders from Buyer or Buyer’s customers for the Products through Supplier’s JD Edwards enterprise resource planning system (the “Supplier ERP System”) on behalf of Buyer, (ii) Supplier will assemble Products ordered by Buyer or Buyer’s customers through such intake process in accordance with Supplier’s existing sales, inventory, and operations planning process (“SIOP”), unless otherwise directed by Buyer,
(iii) census demand (output from SIOP) will then be used to generate an optimal production plan, and (iv) Supplier will ship and deliver such Products in accordance with Section 2.6 and customer daily and weekly requirements. Supplier shall provide pricing as calculated in this Agreement in written weekly sales and expense reports (“Reports”), which Reports will include information regarding the prior week’s customer orders, Materials (as defined below) purchases and Materials Costs, manufacturing output and related Product Price as detailed in Section 3.1, delivery status, quality reports, customer scorecards, and any Excluded Costs (as defined below) incurred and approved by Buyer in accordance with this Agreement to meet the obligations required in this Agreement. “Products” means the products listed on Exhibit A to this Agreement, which may be updated from time to time in accordance with this Section 1.1(a). Either Party, through the Buyer


Lead (as defined below) or Supplier Lead (as defined below) (as applicable), may suggest changes to Products listed on Exhibit A by providing written notice to the other Party, and the Parties will work together in good faith to review and discuss the proposed changes, including Product Price and customer requirements, in accordance with Section 1.3. For changes that require changes to Exhibit A, a revised Exhibit A will be documented in writing and attached to this Agreement.
(b)    The Parties anticipate that the quantities and types of Products to be purchased by Buyer or Buyer’s customers will be consistent with the requirements of the Business (as defined in the Purchase Agreement) as presented in the product forecast model shared with Buyer, subject to updates based on future forecasted demands covered by the Customer’s Supply Agreements (as defined below). The Parties will work together in good faith to update the Product forecast model based on future forecasted demands. Supplier shall purchase and maintain sufficient inventory for the manufacture of the Products based on current and future customer demands covered by the Customer’s Supply Agreements.
(c)    Supplier will comply with the ordering requirements in the applicable Buyer and applicable Buyer customers’ long term supply agreements and purchase orders and each of Buyer’s and Buyer’s customers’ global terms and conditions of purchase, as applicable, with the Supplier that are in effect as of the Effective Date (each, including as amended as provided in the following sentence, a “Customer’s Supply Agreement”). Buyer may amend or revise any Customer’s Supply Agreement after the Effective Date at its discretion; provided, however, no such amendment or revision that affects Supplier’s obligations under this Agreement will be binding on Supplier without Supplier’s prior written consent, which such consent will not be unreasonably withheld, conditioned or delayed.
1.2    Product Specifications. Supplier has developed its manufacturing processes, including in-line and end-of-line testing to ensure that the Products meet required functional specifications prior to shipment (“Product Testing”). Product Testing to ensure functional requirements compliance is built into the production line and is required to be executed for parts and components to move through the assembly process. Supplier will perform Product Testing on the Products and any parts or components incorporated into the Products that fail to perform functionally will be removed and either reworked or scrapped. The Products will be manufactured in accordance with the specifications with which such Products are made as of the Effective Date and in a manner consistent with good manufacturing practices and quality levels for the type of Products provided hereunder unless the Parties jointly approve in writing a specific change to such specifications (with Supplier’s approval of such change not to be unreasonably withheld, conditioned or delayed) (as so changed, collectively, the “Specifications”). The Parties acknowledge and agree that the Specifications applicable to the Products at the Effective Date shall serve as a reference point for the status and condition of each Product manufactured following the Effective Date.
1.3    Supply Management. During the Term, Buyer shall designate a primary point of contact employed by Supplier that communicates with Supplier regarding Buyer’s positions on staffing levels, potential efficiency improvements, and other items Buyer desires to present (the “Buyer Lead”). During the Term, Supplier shall maintain a point of contact to interface with the Buyer Lead on discussion and assistance with Buyer Lead’s proposed initiatives (the “Supplier Lead”). Buyer and Supplier may each change their primary point of contact from time to time by

providing email notice to the other Party regarding same. The Buyer Lead and the Supplier Lead shall maintain regular access to one another while both are present in the Maquiladora to facilitate real-time communication and collaboration. In addition, the Buyer Lead and the Supplier Lead shall meet formally at least weekly, or as otherwise agreed between the Buyer Lead and the Supplier Lead. The Supplier Lead will consider the Buyer Lead’s suggestions in good faith and will provide feedback on suggested impacts to the business and operations as a result of the Buyer Lead’s suggestions, including whether additional costs will be incurred in order to implement each item. Supplier will implement all reasonable items which the Buyer confirms in writing should be implemented after discussion with the Supplier Lead. Buyer will be responsible for increases in costs as a result of any changes made under this Section 1.3. Neither the Buyer Lead nor the Supplier Lead will have the authority to modify or amend the terms of this Agreement.
Section 2.    Order and Shipment.
2.1    Filling Orders.
(a)    Supplier shall fill Buyer’s or Buyer’s customer orders in accordance with Supplier’s historical practice (subject to reasonable updates in accordance with this Agreement that are communicated by Buyer to Supplier) and with delivery timelines consistent with historical order timelines for the Products (in each case, subject to the terms of each Customer’s Supply Agreement). For the avoidance of doubt, Supplier’s historical practice includes the monthly execution of the SIOP process engineered to drive an optimal production plan. Supplier shall notify Buyer in writing of any issues in the customer orders (including with respect to order delivery dates) no later than five (5) business days following Supplier’s receipt of such customer order or the issue arising, whichever is later. Any terms submitted by Buyer which conflict with or are inconsistent with the terms of this Agreement shall not apply to fulfillment of Buyer or Buyer’s customer orders or to payment by Buyer under this Agreement unless both Parties expressly accept such terms in writing.
(b)    Supplier shall manufacture and deliver the Products in the quantities and on the dates requested by Buyer or Buyer’s customers for the period prescribed by each applicable Customer’s Supply Agreement, and otherwise satisfy all other related production and delivery obligations to Buyer and Buyers’ customers, all in accordance with and to the extent provided by the applicable Customer’s Supply Agreement.
(c)    During the Term, for Buyer’s customers that maintain a supplier scorecard, Supplier shall endeavor to maintain substantially equivalent or better scores for items within Supplier’s control as compared to the six (6) month period immediately prior to the Effective Date. To the extent Buyer has concerns with Supplier performance hereunder and the supplier scorecards show a material degradation in scores for items within Supplier’s control, Supplier will use continuous efforts to improve performance to align with historical scores.
2.2    Modifications and Change Orders. Buyer shall provide Supplier with written notice of any proposed modifications to the Specifications, quantities, or delivery schedules. Upon receiving such written notice, the Buyer Lead and Supplier Lead will communicate regarding the anticipated implications of Buyer’s proposed modifications, including any impact on Product Price, engineering, production timelines, or other operational factors. Upon Buyer’s confirmation

2.3    Covered Tools. Subject to Section 10, Supplier will keep the tools located in the Maquiladora that were acquired by Buyer under the Purchase Agreement (the “Covered Tools”) separate from Supplier’s other tools and will only use the Covered Tools to manufacture the Products. Supplier will cover the costs for, and will cause to be performed, all scheduled and routine maintenance of the Covered Tools in accordance with historical maintenance schedules and in a manner consistent with good repair and maintenance practices to keep the Covered Tools in good working condition (“Routine Maintenance”). In the event a Covered Tool becomes unusable or the output of a Covered Tool is materially diminished, Supplier will promptly, but in no event later than two (2) business days, notify Buyer in writing of such condition and promptly provide Buyer an estimate to repair or replace the Covered Tool in order to meet the obligations of this Agreement. Upon written approval by Buyer of the estimate (if applicable), Supplier shall proceed with the repair or replacement and charge Buyer for the repair or replacement pursuant to Section 3 as an Excluded Cost, except that if a Covered Tool becomes unusable or the output of a Covered Tool is materially diminished solely as a result of either (a) the Supplier’s or the Maquiladora’s failure to perform, or failure to cause to be performed, Routine Maintenance or (b) Supplier’s, the Maquiladora’s, or any of their respective agent’s, employee’s and/or representative’s misuse of the Covered Tools or negligence, Supplier will promptly repair or replace such Covered Tool at its own expense. Supplier will not use any tools other than the Covered Tools to manufacture Products and Supplier will have no obligation to manufacture Products if any Covered Tools used to manufacture such Products are non-operational or are removed from the Maquiladora; provided, however, that (i) Supplier has given Buyer advance notice of any non-operational Covered Tools and a reasonable period to have such Covered Tools repaired or replaced, it being agreed that Supplier will reasonably cooperate with such repair and replacement process and (ii) Supplier shall not be excused from performance hereunder where Supplier is required to repair or replace a Covered Tool at its own expense. The Parties intend for Supplier and the Maquiladora’s right to use the Covered Tools to satisfy the requirements of Article 181 of the Mexican Income Tax Law (Ley del Impuesto sobre la Renta). The Covered Tools are supplied by Buyer to Supplier and the Maquiladora, derived from the commercial relationship with Supplier and in compliance with the provisions of Article 181, section IV, second paragraph of the Mexican Income Tax Law.
2.4    Use of Existing Materials. The Parties acknowledge and agree that Buyer acquired certain Materials (as defined below) and packaging under the Purchase Agreement that were allocated for production of Products in the Maquiladora (“Existing Materials”). Supplier shall use the Existing Materials to produce Products, and will purchase Materials and packaging as provided in Sections 2.5 and 2.7, respectively in accordance with the SIOP process. Given the assignment of material supply contracts for Materials for Products under the Purchase Agreement (the “Supply Contracts”), Buyer has the right to negotiate and update the Supply Contracts with such suppliers under which Supplier will purchase Materials on Buyer’s behalf under this Agreement.

2.5    Purchase of Materials; Title.
(a)    In addition to the Existing Materials, Supplier will procure all raw materials, components, sub-components, subassemblies, parts and supplies necessary and required for the manufacture and assembly of the Products pursuant to the Supply Contracts, including as modified by Buyer hereunder (collectively, the “Materials”), giving consideration to Existing Material inventory levels, unless otherwise directed by Buyer to increase or reduce such levels in accordance with Section 1.3. Supplier shall arrange for the procurement of the Materials to the Maquiladora at Buyer’s sole cost and expense. Buyer will own and control the Supply Contracts following the Effective Date, and may update any terms of any of the Supply Contracts (e.g., to reduce costs) by negotiating with the applicable supplier and providing written notification of changes to Supplier. Following such written notification, Supplier shall update terms and conditions in the Supplier ERP System and proceed to order under the updated terms and conditions, passing along the change in Product Pricing as outlined in the Reports. Supplier shall utilize recent historical practice related to inbound freight in determining freight costs. Buyer will pay all freight and delivery costs incurred by Supplier or its Affiliates in procuring and delivering the Materials and packaging to the Maquiladora, including any applicable taxes, or other shipping expenses actually incurred by Supplier and its Affiliates. Supplier will submit amounts owed by Buyer in the Reports as procurement activities occur, and Buyer shall pay all amounts owed in accordance with Sections 3.1 and 3.2. Supplier shall promptly, but in any event no later than five
(5) business days, notify Buyer in writing if Supplier becomes aware that a third-party Materials supplier has breached the terms of the applicable Supply Contract in a manner that would impact production under this Agreement. Notwithstanding anything herein to the contrary, the Parties agree that Supplier will have no liability to Buyer or any other Person (as defined in the Purchase Agreement) in the event that a non-Affiliate supplier of Materials breaches or terminates any contract under which Materials are supplied or otherwise discontinues or refuses to supply Materials to Supplier (collectively, a “Materials Supplier Non-Performance”) during the Term unless such breach, termination or refusal to supply is caused solely by Supplier’s action(s).
(b)    The Parties acknowledge and agree that Buyer will (subject to payment of the costs for Materials pursuant to Section 2.5(a)) retain title to, and bear the risk of loss or damage to, all Materials and Products while such Materials and Products are in Supplier’s custody; provided that Buyer will not be responsible for any loss or damage to the Existing Materials or the Materials to the extent such loss or damage results from Supplier’s, the Maquiladora’s or any of their respective agent’s or representative’s misuse of such Existing Materials or Materials or negligence. During the Term, Supplier shall use reasonable efforts to (a) store and handle the Existing Materials and the Materials in a secure, organized and protective manner, (b) prevent loss, theft or damage to the Existing Materials and the Materials, and (c) maintain the Existing Materials and the Materials separate from its own inventory.

Buyer shall pay for shipping charges, insurance and taxes related to the Products and any duties and charges of any kind imposed by any governmental authority with respect to, or measured by, the manufacture, sale, shipment or use of the Products. To the extent that shipment of the Products is delayed due to any act or omission of Supplier, including

Supplier will purchase packaging for shipment of the Products (after exhaustion of all packaging purchased by Buyer under the Purchase Agreement that was allocated to the Maquiladora for production, if any) and Supplier will package Products for shipment using Supplier’s standard methods for packaging such Products, and will charge the costs of the packaging as provided in Section 2.5. Supplier will use reasonable efforts to ensure packaging costs are in accordance with historical standards and communicate to Buyer in writing if any material increases in costs or deviation of materials is anticipated.
2.8    Access to Maquiladora.
(a)    Supplier agrees to grant to Buyer reasonable access to the Maquiladora to the extent permitted under applicable law. All access by Buyer personnel must take place during Supplier’s regular business hours and shall not unreasonably interfere with the operation of Supplier’s and the Maquiladora’s business. While on-site, Buyer must abide by all of Supplier’s and the Maquiladora’s health, safety, security and environmental guidelines and requirements that are communicated to Buyer.
(b)    If (i) greater than five percent (5%) of any Product shipped to Buyer’s customers under this Agreement, calculated in accordance with historical methodologies, fail to meet required Specifications or (ii) Buyer’s customers require Buyer to perform a root cause analysis on any Products supplied under this Agreement, then, in each case, the Parties shall cooperate in good faith to promptly conduct a root cause analysis to identify the underlying cause of such defect or quality issue. Upon written notification of (i) or (ii) above, Buyer and Supplier will draft a written process to identify the root cause of the defect or quality issue, which will include expected timing or each step and resources required to remedy the issue. Buyer will engage with affected customers to negotiate the cost of the quality related issue. To the extent that the failure is caused by a quality issue related to third-party supplied Materials, product design, manufacturing design or any non-workmanship related issue, Buyer will be responsible for any increased quality related costs to the customers. In the event of remanufacturing or incremental quantity requirements, Buyer and Supplier will agree in writing to timing and delivery of Products, including Excluded Costs or changes to the Product Price for the applicable Products, if any, to deliver the Products. Each Party will promptly notify the other in writing if such Party reasonably believes that a recall, field alert, product withdrawal or field correction with respect to any of the Products may be necessary or advisable. Buyer and Supplier will reasonably cooperate with each other with respect to product recalls, including compliance with product recall obligations in the applicable Customer’s Supply Agreement. Notwithstanding the foregoing, Supplier’s obligations to perform a root cause analysis will end on the date Supplier commences the dismantling of the Covered Tools to move to the Destination Location in accordance with Section 10.2; provided that to the extent Supplier continues to possess applicable data, Products, tooling or personnel, Supplier

2.9    Order Banking. Upon Buyer’s request, Supplier and Buyer shall meet to discuss Materials orders, labor planning, warehousing, and other logistics, which will all be subject to the terms and conditions of this Agreement upon mutual agreement of the Parties. Buyer and Supplier will agree in writing to bank build scheduling prior to execution of a bank build. The Parties acknowledge and agree that the Product Price covers incremental costs from order banking (e.g., storage and ordinary course production), although Buyer will be required to pay for Materials (other than Existing Materials) and packaging for order banked Products.
Section 3.    Price and Payment Terms.
3.1    Purchase Price.
(a)    Buyer shall pay to Supplier (i) reasonably documented Materials and packaging costs, as described in Sections 2.4, 2.5, and 2.7 (“Materials Costs”), (ii) the Product Price, (iii) shipping costs incurred in accordance with Section 2.6, and, (iv) if applicable, reasonably documented Excluded Costs.
(b)    “Product Price” means the price for each Product as set forth on Exhibit A. The Product Price for each Product will remain fixed for three (3) years following the Effective Date. Thereafter, the Parties will agree to split increases to Product Prices based on the annual increase in Mexico CPI after the initial three (3) year period (as compared to the Effective Date with respect to the first annual adjustment) with Buyer and Supplier each absorbing half of such inflationary increases after such period on a going forward basis.
(c)    “Excluded Costs” means, as approved by Buyer in writing from time to time, costs related to non-ordinary activities associated with the activities under this Agreement, including machinery and equipment maintenance other than ordinary course maintenance, unexpected and non-ordinary course finance, HR or IT requests or requirements (including as requested by the Buyer Lead), and quality-related issues, including expediting shipments and internal or external sorting (other than to the extent caused by Supplier’s breach of Section 1.2), in each case, not otherwise covered under this Agreement. Following approval by Buyer, the Excluded Costs will be provided monthly to Buyer in Reports and invoiced pursuant to Section
3.2. All services performed and billed as Excluded Costs will be performed by Supplier and/or Maquiladora, as applicable, and charged to Buyer based on the actual hours worked by Supplier’s and the Maquiladora’s personnel (without any markup) and the actual cost of any materials, components, tools, or equipment used or consumed in the performance of such services, including any applicable handling or procurement fees.
