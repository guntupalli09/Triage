WHEREAS, Ashford Trust, the Operating Partnership and Advisor entered into a Third Amended and Restated Advisory Agreement (as amended, the “Existing Advisory Agreement”) dated and effective on March 12, 2024, pursuant to which the Advisor agreed to perform certain advisory services identified in such agreement, on behalf of, and subject to the supervision of, the board of directors of Ashford Trust (the “Board of Directors”), in exchange for the compensation set forth therein;

WHEREAS, the Advisor is willing to continue to provide such services to the Company on the terms set forth herein;
NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants set forth in this Agreement, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which is hereby acknowledged, and intending to be legally bound hereby, the parties agree as follows:
