BINDING LETTER OF INTENT
Strategic Joint Venture, Revenue Sharing, and Proposed
Acquisition of FORCARA, LLC by Global Technologies, LTD
This Binding Letter of Intent, dated as of June 8, 2026, is entered into by and between Global
Technologies, LTD, a Delaware corporation, including its subsidiaries and affiliates where applicable
(“GTLL” or the “Company”), and FORCARA, LLC, a limited liability company (““FORCARA”),
together with its current members, managers, and authorized representatives, including William “Bill”
Norton where applicable.
GTLL and FORCARA may be referred to individually as a “Party” and collectively as the “Parties.”
1. Purpose of Letter of Intent
The purpose of this Binding Letter of Intent is to set forth the principal terms under which the Parties
intend to immediately begin a strategic business relationship and complete a definitive transaction
pursuant to which FORCARA will become part of the GTLL operating platform.
The Parties acknowledge that FORCARA was created and developed in connection with the business
opportunity arising from the former Genesis Four automotive repair software and services platform.
FORCARA seeks to modernize, operate, expand, and monetize the former Genesis Four platform,
including the existing automotive repair shop client base, software relationships, advisory opportunities,
marketing systems, coaching services, tax advisory opportunities, employment leasing opportunities, and
related small business service offerings.
The Parties further acknowledge that GTLL is currently operating other revenue-generating subsidiaries
and is in the process of restructuring its capital structure, preferred stock designations, common stock
authorizations, and potential reverse stock split strategy. As a result, the Parties desire to establish a
binding framework that allows GTLL and FORCARA to begin working together immediately, while
reserving final equity conversion mechanics for a definitive agreement to be completed on or before June
30, 2026.
2. Business Rationale
The Parties believe the FORCARA opportunity may provide meaningful value to GTLL and its
stockholders by adding an operating platform with existing annualized revenue, an established automotive
repair shop client base, and the potential to expand revenue per client through additional
technology-enabled services.

 

 
 

 

The primary business objectives include:
1. Preserving and stabilizing existing Genesis Four / FORCARA client revenue;
2. Reducing or halting customer attrition;
3. Modernizing outdated software into more current technology and Al-enabled learning, training,
reporting, and client management applications;
4. Expanding the service stack offered to existing and future clients;
5. Increasing revenue per client through advisory, marketing, coaching, tax advisory, employment
leasing, and business optimization services;
6. Using GTLL’s public company platform, strategic management support, reporting discipline,
capital planning, and future funding opportunities to support FORCARA’s growth; and
7. Creating a fair equity-based transaction for the current FORCARA members while protecting
GTLL’s existing stockholders and capital structure.

4. Immediate Joint Venture and Revenue Sharing Arrangement
Effective as of June 8, 2026, the Parties agree to begin operating under an interim joint venture and
revenue-sharing arrangement while definitive agreements are prepared.
The purpose of the interim arrangement is to allow GILL to immediately provide strategic management
support, public company oversight, financial reporting assistance, business development support,
accounting coordination, capital planning, marketing support, and other growth-related services to
FORCARA, while also allowing GTLL to begin participating in the revenue generated by FORCARA.

 

 
 

 

4.1 Interim Revenue Sharing
Beginning as of the effective date of this Letter of Intent, FORCARA shall report all gross revenue,
customer receipts, recurring revenue, new client revenue, contract revenue, and related service revenue to
GTLL on a weekly basis.
Unless otherwise agreed in writing, the Parties intend to allocate FORCARA revenue during the interim
period as follows:
1. FORCARA shall retain amounts necessary to pay direct platform costs, vendor costs, software
costs, customer support costs, and other direct cost of revenue obligations;
2. FORCARA shall retain amounts necessary to fund reasonable ordinary-course operating expenses
approved by the Parties;
3. Net revenue, gross profit, or EBITDA, as defined in the definitive joint venture or management
agreement, shall be shared between FORCARA and GTLL pursuant to a mutually agreed
schedule;
4. Until such schedule is finalized, the Parties agree that GILL shall be entitled to receive, accrue,
or be allocated a management fee and/or revenue participation for the strategic support provided
under this Letter of Intent.
The Parties may elect to document the interim revenue arrangement through a separate Joint Venture
Agreement, Management Services Agreement, Revenue Sharing Agreement, or Intercompany
Services Agreement, which may be executed simultaneously with, or shortly after, this Letter of Intent.
4.2 Suggested Interim Revenue Formula
For purposes of beginning the relationship immediately, the Parties agree to use the following interim
framework unless replaced by a definitive revenue-sharing agreement:
FORCARA shall report weekly gross revenue and collections to GTLL;
FORCARA shall identify direct cost of revenue, platform cost, and required operating expenses;
GTLL shall be entitled to accrue or receive a management fee equal to $12,500 per month,
prorated from the effective date of this Letter of Intent, for strategic management, reporting,
capital planning, and public company support;
e After direct costs and approved operating expenses, GTLL and FORCARA shall share EBITDA
or net operating profit on a 50/50 basis, unless otherwise modified in definitive agreements;
e Any amounts not paid currently may be accrued as an intercompany payable or
transaction-related obligation.
This interim structure is intended to create immediate alignment while preserving flexibility for the final
acquisition structure.

8. Proposed Valuation
The Parties currently estimate the initial value of the FORCARA / Genesis Four platform opportunity at
approximately Two Hundred Fifty Thousand Dollars ($250,000).
This valuation is based on:
1. Current annualized revenue estimated at approximately Three Hundred Thousand Dollars
($300,000);

3. The potential to retain and stabilize the current client base;
4. The opportunity to increase revenue per client;

10. Milestone-Based Equity Structure
The Parties intend that the proposed FORCARA preferred equity shall be earned, issued, released, or
converted based on a combination of closing and future performance milestones.
The definitive agreements may include milestones substantially similar to the following:
10.1 Closing Milestone
A portion of the preferred equity shall be earned upon execution of definitive agreements and
confirmation that FORCARA has the contractual right to operate, manage, own, acquire, or control the
Genesis Four platform, customer base, revenue relationships, and related business opportunity.
10.2 Revenue Retention Milestone
A portion of the preferred equity shall be earned upon FORCARA maintaining annualized revenue of not
less than approximately $300,000 for a mutually agreed period following closing.
10.3 Revenue Growth Milestone
A portion of the preferred equity shall be earned upon FORCARA achieving increased annualized
revenue targets, which may include milestones such as:

10.4 Software Modernization Milestone
A portion of the preferred equity may be earned upon completion of a mutually agreed technology
modernization plan, including Al-enabled learning applications, updated client-facing software tools,
management dashboards, reporting systems, client portals, or other platform enhancements.
10.5 EBITDA / Profitability Milestone
A portion of the preferred equity may be earned upon FORCARA achieving positive EBITDA, recurring

including advisory services, marketing systems, coaching, tax advisory, employment leasing, or other
business optimization services.

authorization of additional common stock, and the creation or designation of one or more new preferred
stock classes.

From the date of this Letter of Intent through the earlier of closing or termination of this Letter of Intent,
FORCARA and its members, managers, officers, and representatives shall not solicit, negotiate, accept, or
enter into any competing transaction involving the sale, transfer, merger, acquisition, assignment,

 

 
 

 

licensing, management, or control of FORCARA, the Genesis Four platform opportunity, or any material

16. Confidentiality
The Parties agree to maintain the confidentiality of all non-public information exchanged in connection
with this Letter of Intent, the proposed transaction, FORCARA’s business, GTLL’s capital structure,
customer information, financial information, and strategic plans.
No Party shall disclose the terms of this Letter of Intent or the proposed transaction except to its attorneys,
accountants, auditors, advisors, officers, directors, members, managers, or as otherwise required by law,
regulation, public company disclosure obligations, or securities compliance requirements.
17. Public Company Disclosure
FORCARA acknowledges that GTLL is a public company and may be required to disclose material
agreements, business combinations, revenue arrangements, related-party matters, preferred equity
issuances, or other transaction terms in public filings, press releases, OTC Markets disclosures, SEC
reports, or other public company communications.
GTLL shall have the right to determine, in consultation with its counsel and advisors, whether and when
public disclosure is required.
18. Expenses
Each Party shall bear its own legal, accounting, advisory, due diligence, and transaction-related expenses
unless otherwise agreed in writing.
19, Termination
This Letter of Intent may be terminated:
1. By mutual written agreement of the Parties;
2. By GTLL if due diligence is not satisfactory;
3. By either Party if definitive agreements are not executed by June 30, 2026, unless extended by
mutual written agreement;
4. By either Party upon material breach by the other Party, following written notice and a reasonable
opportunity to cure;
5. By GTLL if FORCARA cannot demonstrate sufficient rights to operate, manage, acquire, or
control the Genesis Four platform and related client relationships; or
6. By either Party if the transaction cannot be completed due to legal, regulatory, corporate,
accounting, or securities compliance limitations.
