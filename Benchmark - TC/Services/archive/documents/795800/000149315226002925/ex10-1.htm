CONSULTING SERVICES AGREEMENT
THIS CONSULTING SERVICES AGREEMENT dated as of January 15, 2026
between NexMetals Mining Corp., a corporation existing under the laws of the Province of British
Columbia (the “Company") and Elkam Consulting Ltd. (the "Consultant") as an independent
contractor to provide the personal services of Sean Whiteford (the "Principal") to perform
consulting services for the Company (this “Agreement”).
WHEREAS the Consultant is a party to an agreement with Premium Resources
International Limited (“PRIL”), a subsidiary of the Company, dated October 17, 2023, as amended
on April 17, 2025 (the “2023 Agreement”):
WHEREAS the Company wishes the Principal to assume the role as the Company’s
Chief Executive Officer (“CEO”) effective January 15, 2026, and the Consultant, on behalf of the
Principal, has agreed to do so on the terms and subject to the conditions set forth in this Agreement:
and
WHEREAS the Company and the Consultant hereby agree that this Agreement will
replace the 2023 Agreement in its entirety effective January 1, 2026 (the “Effective Date”).
NOW, THEREFORE. in consideration of the respective covenants, promises and
obligations set forth herein, the parties agree as follows:
ARTICLE 1
TERM
1.1 Term
The Consultant’s engagement as an independent contractor under the terms of this
Agreement will commence on, and be effective as of, the Effective Date and will continue until
terminated as provided herein.
ARTICLE 2
SERVICES
2.1 Services & Primary Contact
During the term of this Agreement, the Consultant will perform the services as more
particularly described on Schedule “A” (the "Services"). The Consultant will make the Principal
available to perform the Services and will not permit any other person to perform the Services
without the prior written consent of the Company. The Company's primary contact with the
Consultant will be the Principal.
2.2 Board of Directors
The Principal will report to the Board of Directors of the Company (the "Board").
The Principal shall hold such other office or offices of the Company as may from time to time
reasonably be required by the Board to facilitate the performance of the Services by the Principal.

 

 

 

 

2.3 Location of Principal
The Principal shall provide the Services principally from a home office at his
residence in Chagrin Falls, Ohio or as otherwise provided by the Principal. The Principal will be
required to travel frequently for business purposes to the operations of the Company and elsewhere
to engage with directors, officers and employees of the Company, shareholders, potential
investors, regulatory authorities, governmental agencies and other third parties.
2.4 Authorizations
The Consultant and the Company shall do all such things as may be necessary or
advisable on their respective parts to obtain and maintain all visas, work permits and other
authorizations or documentation required to enable the Principal to travel freely between Canada
and the United States, or between either of them and the Republic of Botswana or between any of
them and Barbados, or to or from any other jurisdiction, for the purposes of performing the
Services to the Company hereunder.
2.5 Responsibilities of Principal
The Consultant shall ensure that the Principal shall devote his best efforts to the
business and affairs of the Company in performing the Services and during the Term will not
engage in any other business, profession or occupation which would conflict or interfere with the
performance of the Services either directly or indirectly. The Principal will not be permitted to act
or serve as a director, trustee, committee member or principal of any type of business, civic or
charitable organization which would conflict or interfere with the performance of the Services
without the Board's prior written consent, which consent shall be exercised in the Board's
discretion.
2.6 Responsibility of Consultant for Principal
The Consultant will be responsible for all of the acts and omissions of the Principal
as if the things he does (or fails to do) were things the Consultant did (or failed to do). The
Consultant will also: (a) be responsible to ensure that the Principal is trained and competent: (b)
supervise and control the Principal: (c) exercise exclusive responsibility for the Principal: (d) pay
and treat the Principal in accordance with the Consultant’s obligations under the law; and (e) direct
the Principal to follow the terms of this Agreement.
2.7 Performance
The Consultant will, and will cause the Principal to, perform the Services in a
competent, professional, efficient and ethical manner that promotes the interests, goodwill and
business of the Company, at all times in compliance with those of the Company policies and
guidelines that may be disclosed as applicable to the Consultant from time to time as well the
highest standards and practices commonly expected of qualified and experienced providers of
sumilar services. The Consultant will ensure that the Principal devotes good faith, sufficient effort

Commencing on the Effective Date, the Company shall pay the Consultant a
monthly fee of US$25,850 (the "Monthly Fee"), subject to such increases as may be approved
from time to time by the Board.
The Monthly Fee shall be reviewed annually, in the month of March, by the
Compensation Committee and the Board. Any increase in the Monthly Fee approved by the Board
as a result of any such review shall be effective retroactive to January | of the year in which such
approval is granted.
The Consultant will invoice the Company on a monthly basis, with payment of such
invoices being paid within fifteen business (15) days of the Company’s receipt of such invoice,
plus applicable taxes.
The Consultant will be solely responsible for: (a) determining and making payment
of Consultant and Principal’s own federal, state and local income taxes and the Company will not
withhold any such taxes from amounts payable to Consultant by the Company ; and (b)

employment and any tax related payments and remittances for the Consultant and the Principal as
required by any law. Consultant shall be solely and exclusively responsible and liable for all
