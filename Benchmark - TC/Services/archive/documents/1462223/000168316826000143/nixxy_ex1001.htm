MASTER DISTRIBUTION AGREEMENT

 

THIS MASTER DISTRIBUTION AGREEMENT (this “Agreement”), dated as of December 31, 2025 (the “Execution Date”), is made and entered into by and between Nixxy, Inc., a Nevada corporation (“Nixxy”), and CognoGroup, Inc., a Colorado corporation and wholly owned subsidiary of Nixxy (“CognoGroup”). Each of Nixxy and CognoGroup may be referred to herein individually as a “Party” and collectively as the “Parties.”

 

RECITALS

 

WHEREAS, the Board of Directors of Nixxy (the “Nixxy Board”) has determined that it is advisable and in the best interests of Nixxy’s stockholders to separate the CognoGroup Business from that of Nixxy’s standing alone, creating two independent publicly traded companies;

 

WHEREAS, the Parties contemplate that the CognoGroup Business will be transferred to CognoGroup (the “Separation”) as provided for in the Separation Agreement;

 

WHEREAS, the Parties contemplate that following the CognoGroup Transfer, Nixxy shall distribute all of the CognoGroup Shares held by Nixxy to holders of Nixxy’s Shares (as of the Record Date) on the Distribution Date, on a pro rata basis (the “Distribution”);

 

WHEREAS, the Parties intend in this Agreement to set forth the principal corporate transactions required to effect the Distribution and certain other agreements governing various matters relating to the Distribution and the relationship of Nixxy, CognoGroup and the members of their respective Groups following the Distribution; and

 

WHEREAS, Nixxy and CognoGroup acknowledge that this Agreement, the Separation Agreement and the Ancillary Agreements represent the integrated agreement of Nixxy and CognoGroup relating to the Distribution, are entered into simultaneously and would not have been entered into independently.

 

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual agreements, provisions and covenants contained in this Agreement, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, the Parties, intending to be legally bound, hereby agree as follows:
