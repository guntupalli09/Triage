INTERIM COMMERCIALIZATION AND REVENUE SHARE AGREEMENT
i erim Commercialization and Revenue Share Agreement (this “Agreement”) is made as of
Deans (the “Effective Date’), by and between WELLGISTICS HEALTH, INC., a
Delaware corporation with its principal place of business at 3000 Bayport Drive, Suite 950, Tampa,
FL 33607 (“Weligistics’), and KARE PHARMTECH, LLC, a Florida limited liability company with
its principal place of business at 5600 Mariner Street., Ste 220, Tampa, FL 33609 (“PharmTech’”).
Wellgistics and PharmTech may be referred to individually as a “Party” and collectively as the
“Parties.”
BACKGROUND:
1. The Parties are currently evaluating a broader strategic relationship involving the
commercialization and distribution of certain pharmaceutical and healthcare products.
2. Pending negotiation of any such broader arrangement, the Parties desire to collaborate
on an interim basis to commercialize certain PharmTech products through the Wellgistics distribution and
pharmacy network, subject to the terms of this Agreement.
TERMS AND CONDITIONS:
NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the Background above and the mutual covenants and
agreements herein contained, the adequacy and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, the
Parties agree as follows:
1, PRODUCT, “Product” means the following PharmTech product(s):
"KARE Verify," a product that provides benefits verification and eligibility verification
services.
including any associated packaging, formulations, trademarks, and related intellectual
property owned or licensed by PharmTech.
2. COMMERCIALIZATION RIGHTS.
2.1 Authorization. PharmTech hereby authorizes Wellgistics, during the Term of this
Agreement, to market, promote, and distribute the Product through:
2.1.1 Wellgistics pharmacy, manufacturers and distribution channels;
2.1.2 affiliated pharmacies and providers; and
2.1.3. telemedicine and digital pharmacy platforms.
2.2 Supply. PharmTech shall be responsible for supplying the Product or arranging for
supply through its approved manufacturers or distributors.
2.3 Non-Exclusivity. This Agreement is non-exclusive. Nothing herein restricts either
Party from commercializing similar products independently or with third parties.
3. REVENUE SHARE.
3.1 Net Revenue Definition. “Net Revenue” means gross revenue actually received
from the sale of the Product, less the following deductions:
3.1.1 refunds and chargebacks;
3.1.2 payment processing fees;
3.1.3. taxes imposed on the sale;

3.1.4 discounts or rebates granted in the ordinary course; and
3.1.5 shipping and logistics costs directly attributable to the sale.
3,2 Revenue Split. Net Revenue generated from sales of the Product under this

3.2.1 50% to Wellgistics; and
3.2.2. 50% to PharmTech.
3.3 Reporting. Within fifteen (15) days after the end of each calendar month, the
selling Party shall provide a written revenue statement showing:
3.3.1 total gross revenue;
3.3.2 deductions;
3.3.3. Net Revenue calculation; and
3.3.4 revenue share payable,
3.4 Payment, Revenue share payments shall be made within fifteen (15) days after
delivery of the applicable monthly statement. Payments subject to receipt of related customer
invoice collections.
4. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY.
4.1 Ownership. PharmTech retains all right, title, and interest in and to:
4.1.1 the Product;
4.1.2 associated formulations;
4.1.3. trademarks;
4.1.4 patents; and
4.1.5 regulatory approvals.
4.2 Platform and Distribution Rights. Wellgistics retains all right, title, and interest in
and to:
4.2.1 _ its distribution systems;
4.2.2 pharmacy network relationships;
4.2.3 technology platforms; and
4.2.4 commercialization infrastructure.
Nothing in this Agreement transfers ownership of intellectual property between the Parties.
5. REGULATORY COMPLIANCE. Each Party shall comply with all applicable laws and
regulations relating to the commercialization and distribution of the Product, including where applicable:
5.1 FDA regulations;
5.2 state pharmacy licensing requirements;
5.3 drug supply chain security laws; and
5.4 applicable healthcare fraud and abuse laws.

Each Party shall be responsible for compliance with laws applicable to its own activities
under this Agreement.
6. TERM AND TERMINATION.
6.1 Term. This Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and continue for
thirty six (36) months unless earlier terminated,
6.2 Termination for Convenience, Either Party may terminate this Agreement the later
of thirty (30) days written notice to the other Party or end user customer contracts.
6.3 Termination for Cause. Either Party may terminate immediately if the other Party
materially breaches this Agreement and fails to cure such breach within fifteen (15) days after
written notice.
6.4 Effect of Termination, Upon termination:
6.4.1 no new Product sales shall be initiated;
6.4.2 existing customer obligations may be fulfilled; and
6.4.3 revenue sharing shall continue for all transactions occurring prior to
termination.
7. INDEMNIFICATION.
7.1 PharmTech Indemnity. PharmTech shall indemnify and hold harmless Wellgistics
from claims arising from:
7.1.1 product defects;
7.1.2 manufacturing issues; and
7.1.3 intellectual property infringement related to the Product.
7.2 Wellgistics Indemnity. Wellgistics shall indemnify and hold harmless PharmTech
from claims arising from:
7.2.1 improper marketing practices;
7.2.2 distribution misconduct; and
7.2.3 violation of applicable laws in commercialization activities.
8. CONFIDENTIALITY. Each Party agrees to maintain the confidentiality of all non-public
business, technical, and commercial information received from the other Party and to use such
information solely for purposes of performing under this Agreement.
9, NO PARTNERSHIP OR JOINT VENTURE, Nothing in this Agreement shall be deemed to
create any partnership, joint venture, agency, or fiduciary relationship between the Parties. Each Party acts
as an independent contractor.
10. ASSIGNMENT. Neither Party may assign this Agreement without the prior written consent
of the other Party, except in connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of substantially all of its assets,
11. GOVERNING LAW. This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with
the laws of the State of Florida, without regard to conflict of law principles.
12. ENTIRE AGREEMENT. This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the
Parties with respect to the subject matter hereof and supersedes all prior discussions or understandings.
