INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY ACQUISITION AND
MANAGEMENT TRANSITION AGREEMENT
This Strategic Intellectual Property Acquisition and Management Transition Agreement
(this Agreement) is made and entered into as of March 17", 2026 (the Effective Date), by and
among:
1. NaturalShrimp Incorporated, a Nevada corporation (the Company);
2. Hydrenesis, Inc., a Florida corporation (Hydrenesis); and
3. David Antelo, an individual (Antelo)
The Company, Hydrenesis, and Antelo are referred to individually as a Party and collectively as
the Parties
1. Recitals
A. The Company is a public company whose common stock is quoted or traded under the symbol
“SHMP” (or such other symbol as may apply from time to time).
B. Hydrenesis owns certain intellectual property and related technology assets that the Parties
believe can be commercialized through the Company.
C. The Company owes Hydrenesis $1,034,112 (the Hydrenesis Claim), and the Parties desire to
convert the Hydrenesis Claim into equity at Closing (as defined below) as part of the transactions
contemplated herein.

5. Actions at Closing
5.1 Exchange of Legacy Obligations and Issuance of Series L Preferred Stock. The Legacy
Obligations set forth on Exhibit D and E shall have been exchange for Series L Preferred Stock in
accordance with the Capitalization Schedule.
5.2 Release of Escrow Documents. All documents delivered to the Escrow Holder shall be
released to their respective beneficiaries including but not limited to intellectual property
assignments, board resignations, board appointments and the surrender of any Series A Preferred
Stock or Series F Preferred Stock delivered in exchange for Series L Preferred Stock
5.3 Equity Issuance and Milestone Economics.
(a) At Closing, the Company shall issue to Hydrenesis equity securities representing twenty-five
percent (25%) of the Company’s fully diluted capitalization, as reflected on the Capitalization
Schedule, in consideration of the Transferred IP and conversion of the Hydrenesis Claim.

 

 
 

 

(b) At Closing, the Company shall issue to Antelo equity securities representing fifteen percent
(15%) of the Company’s fully diluted capitalization, as reflected on the Capitalization Schedule,
(c) Upon achievement of the Milestone Events, the Company shall issue additional equity
securities to Hydrenesis representing up to an additional fifteen percent (15%) of the Company’s
fully diluted capitalization, and shall issue additional equity securities to David Antelo
representing up to an additional ten percent (10%) of the Company’s fully diluted capitalization,
in each case as set forth in the Milestone Schedule and subject to adjustment for issuances approved
in accordance with this Agreement. Any equity issued upon achievement of a Milestone Event
shall be calculated as a percentage of the Company’s fully diluted capitalization as of the date of
issuance of such equity, after giving effect to all issuances, conversions, and dilutive events
occurring prior thereto.
(d) The form, class, and series of equity issued pursuant to this Section 4.6 shall be as set forth on
the Capitalization Schedule or the Milestone Schedule, as applicable. The Series P Preferred Stock
and Series P-2 Preferred Stock shall contain a covenant that such stock shall not be converted into
Common Stock until such time as at least 75% of the Series L Preferred Stock shall have been
converted into Common Stock.
6. Office and Director Appointment and Escrowed Resignations
6.1 Current Office and Director Resignation. On the Effective Date, each current director and
officer (the “Prior Team”) shall execute an irrevocable resignation which shall take effect
immediately and David Antelo shall be appointed as sole officer and director until the date of the
Closing.
6.2 Antelo Resignation. On the Effective Date, Antelo shall deliver into escrow a signed
resignation pursuant to which Antelo resigns from all positions held by him. The escrowed
resignation shall be released automatically if the transaction is terminated
6.3 Prior Officer Appointments. On the Effective Date, Antelo shall deliver into escrow board

released automatically if the transaction is terminated
6.4 Board Composition at Closing. At Closing, the Board shall consist of three (3) directors:
Antelo, and two (2) directors to be named before closing.

8.1 Outside Date Termination. If Closing has not occurred by the Outside Date, this Agreement
terminates automatically unless Antelo extends the Outside Date in writing.
8.2 Unilateral Extension. Antelo may extend the Outside Date one or more times, in writing, for
an aggregate extension period of up to 30 days. Extension does not waive any rights or cure any
breaches unless expressly stated. Any extension by Antelo shall not require the consent of the
Company, any stockholder, or any legacy security holder, and shall not constitute a waiver of any
condition, breach, or obligation.
8.3 Termination for Cause. Antelo may terminate immediately upon written notice if the
Company materially breaches Sections 7 or 8, or if any material representation becomes untrue, or
if the Company takes actions reasonably likely to impair Closing.
8.4 Effect of Termination. Upon termination:
(a) no IP transfers and no license is granted;
(b) escrowed resignations shall not be released;
(c) the Parties remain bound by the restrictions set forth in Section 9, below, except that the
Company shall file a form 8-K indicating the termination of this Agreement.
8.5 Injunctive Relief. The Parties acknowledge that breach may cause irreparable harm and agree
that injunctive relief is an appropriate remedy.
