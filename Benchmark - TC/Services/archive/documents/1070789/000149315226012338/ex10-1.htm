HYORC CORPORATION (OTCQB: HYOR)
BINDING PROJECT DEVELOPMENT AND TECHNOLOGY
AGREEMENT
Date: Monday, March 23, 2026
Confidential - Subject to NDA
1. PARTIES
This Agreement is entered into between HyOrc Corporation (OTCQB: HYOR), a corporation
organised under the laws of the United States with its registered address at 3050 Post Oak
Boulevard, Suite 510-Q60, Houston, Texas 77 056, USA (“HyOrc’), and ONENERGY
GREENPOWER EAD and ON ENERGY EOOD, companies incorporated under the laws of
Bulgaria with their registered address at 58 Bulgaria Blvd., Entrance C, Floor 6, Office 23,
Sofia 1680, Bulgaria (together, the “OnEnergy Group”).
HyOrc and the OnEnergy Group are hereinafter referred to individually as a “Party” and
collectively as the “Parties”.
2. PURPOSE
The purpose of this Agreement is to establish a binding framework governing the
collaboration between the Parties in connection with:
(a) the preparation and submission of an application to the European Union Innovation
Fund; and
(b) the potential development, financing, construction, and operation of a waste-to-
methanol facility (Stage 3) forming part of the OnEnergy integrated platform.
The Parties acknowledge that this Agreement is intended to enable rapid alignment for the
purposes of the Innovation Fund submission while preserving flexibility for the negotiation
of detailed commercial terms at a later stage.
3. PROJECT SCOPE AND DESCRIPTION
For the avoidance of doubt, HyOrc’s scope of involvement under this Agreement is strictly
limited to Stage 3 of the Project, being the thermochemical conversion of RDF into
methanol.
The Project is based on the following indicative parameters:
- Annual RDF input: approximately 50,000 tonnes
- Operating period: approximately 330 days per year

- Daily RDF processing capacity: approximately 150-155 tonnes per day
The final plant configuration, including detailed engineering design, performance
parameters, and methanol output capacity, shall be determined during the engineering
phase and agreed between the Parties in subsequent definitive agreements.
4. ROLE OF HYORC
HyOrc shall act as the technology partner for Stage 3 and shall be responsible for developing
and providing the technical basis for the Project.
HyOrc’s responsibilities shall include:
(a) preparation of the process design and technical configuration;
(b) development of technical documentation required for the Innovation Fund submission;
(c) provision of input into CAPEX, OPEX, and operational modelling;
(d) participation in engineering development, commissioning, and optimisation; and
(e) provision of ongoing technical support and performance optimisation input.
The Parties acknowledge that HyOrc’s technology and technical input form the basis of the
Stage 3 concept.
5. INNOVATION FUND SUBMISSION
HyOrc shall lead the preparation of the technical components of the Innovation Fund
application relating to Stage 3.
HyOrc shall perform such work using reasonable skill, care, and diligence consistent with
professional standards expected for projects of similar complexity and scale.
The Parties acknowledge that the success of the application is subject to multiple factors,
including but not limited to policy considerations, competitive evaluation, and funding
criteria beyond the technical scope.
6. OUTCOME-BASED EXCLUSIVITY
If HyOrc’s technical solution is accepted, shortlisted, or forms the basis of the Project
development, whether or not funding is awarded, HyOrc shall become the exclusive
technology partner for Stage 3.
OnEnergy shall not, without HyOrc’s prior written consent:
(a) replace HyOrc with an alternative technology provider;
(b) replicate or adapt HyOrc’s technical solution; or
(c) engage third-party technology providers using concepts derived from HyOrc’s work.

If the Innovation Fund application fails primarily due to technical deficiencies attributable
to HyOrc, OnEnergy shall have the right to disengage without penalty.
If the application fails for reasons not attributable to HyOrc’s technical performance, the
Parties shall continue discussions in good faith regarding project implementation.
7. CONTINUITY PRINCIPLE
The Parties agree that all technical work, concepts, and materials developed by HyOrc in
connection with the Project shall not be used to advance the Project without HyOrc’s
continued involvement, unless otherwise agreed in writing.
This provision is intended to ensure continuity between the application phase and any
subsequent development or implementation phase.
8. COMMERCIAL FRAMEWORK
HyOrc shall provide indicative CAPEX, OPEX, and maintenance cost assumptions for the
purposes of the Innovation Fund application.
The Parties agree that detailed commercial terms, including licensing arrangements,
engineering scope, and revenue participation, shall be negotiated in good faith and
formalised in definitive agreements following submission of the application.
9. OPERATIONS AND LIFECYCLE INVOLVEMENT
The Parties acknowledge their intention that HyOrc shall have an ongoing role in the
operational phase of Stage 3, including performance optimisation, technical support, and
system monitoring.
Such involvement shall be formalised in a separate long-term technical services agreement
to be negotiated and agreed between the Parties.
10. BALANCED WITHDRAWAL AND COMPENSATION
If either Party:
(a) withdraws from the Project without reasonable cause following successful technical
validation; or
(b) proceeds with the Project using the other Party’s material contributions without
continued involvement,
then the withdrawing or defaulting Party shall pay the other Party a fixed compensation
amount of €500,000.

The Parties agree that this amount represents a genuine pre-estimate of loss and is
intended to provide certainty and avoid disputes. This amount shall not be subject to
further valuation or adjustment.
11. CONFIDENTIALITY
This Agreement shall be read in conjunction with the existing Non-Disclosure Agreement
between the Parties. In the event of any inconsistency, the provisions of this Agreement
shall prevail in respect of the Project.
12. REGULATORY DISCLOSURE
HyOrc, as a publicly listed company, shall be entitled to disclose the existence and terms of
this Agreement to the extent required or appropriate under applicable securities laws,
including filings with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission.
Such disclosure shall not constitute a breach of this Agreement.
13, ASSIGNMENT
Neither Party may assign or transfer its rights or obligations under this Agreement without
the prior written consent of the other Party, such consent not to be unreasonably withheld.
14, TERM
This Agreement shall remain in effect until it is replaced by definitive agreements or
terminated by mutual written agreement between the Parties.
15. GOVERNING LAW AND DISPUTE RESOLUTION
This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of England
and Wales.
Any dispute arising out of or in connection with this Agreement shall be finally resolved by
arbitration under the Rules of the International Chamber of Commerce (ICC), with the seat
of arbitration in London and proceedings conducted in English.
