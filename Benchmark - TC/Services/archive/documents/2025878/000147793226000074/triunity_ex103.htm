EXHIBIT 10.3

Asset Management Agreement
This Asset Management Agreement (“Agreement”) is entered into as of October 1, 2025, by and between
Kyma Batteries, LLC or its assignee (“Asset Manager”), and BESS Rural Energy Cooperative LCA, and
its subsidiary, DBD Express, LLC (“Owner”), each, individually, as a “Party,” and collectively, as the
“Parties.”
RECITALS
WHEREAS the Owner leases battery energy storage system units (“Facilities”) to various
customers (“End Customers”) described in further detail in Exhibit A hereto;
WHEREAS Asset Manager provides rental management and customer interfacing services:
WHEREAS Owner desires that Asset Manager provide asset management services to Owner, and
Asset Manager is willing to provide asset management services for such Owner with respect to the
Facilities, described in further detail in Exhibit A hereto, all subject and pursuant to the terms and conditions
of this Agreement;
WHEREAS Owner and Asset Manager both acknowledge the intent of this Agreement is to
comply with applicable law and U.S. Treasury regulations and guidance regarding the investment tax credit
(“ITC”) under Sections 38(b)(1), 46, and 48(a) of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986, as amended (“Tax
Code”); and
NOW, THEREFORE, for and in consideration of the mutual promises and covenants contained
in this Agreement and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which is
hereby acknowledged, Owner and Asset Manager agree as follows:
AGREEMENT
1. ASSET MANAGER SCOPE OF WORK:
a. Appointment: Owner appoints Asset Manager, and Asset Manager accepts such
appointment, to perform the administrative, monitoring, advisory and consulting services
in relation to the affairs of the Owner and as the Owner may reasonably request from time
to time, as described in Exhibit B, and subject to and in accordance with the terms and
provisions of this Agreement (collectively, the “Work’’). In no event shall, shall the Work
performed by Asset Manager create any right, title or interest of the Asset Manager in the
Facilities or in any associated ITCs or other tax benefits.
b. Effective Date: The Asset Manager will begin performing the Work on the date set forth
in the preamble above (“Effective Date’’).
C. Asset Management Services: The Asset Manager agrees to perform Work for the entire
period of the Term.
d. Work Standards: The Asset Manager will perform the Work in accordance with all
applicable laws and with Prudent Industry Standards and the Asset Manager shall devote
such time and efforts to the performance of the Services as the Asset Manager deems
reasonably necessary or appropriate. All Work shall be performed in a manner to establish
and preserve the eligibility of the Facilities for ITCs in accordance with applicable law,

 

 
 

 

 

including the Tax Code and U.S. Treasury regulations and guidance, as amended from time
to time (the “ITC Eligibility”).
For purposes of this Agreement, “Prudent Industry Standards” means, at a particular time,
in the exercise of reasonable judgment in light of the facts known at the time a decision
was made, those practices, standards, designs, methods, means, techniques, equipment and
acts that would require a person to: (a) perform its duties in good faith and as a reasonably
prudent asset manager with respect to battery storage facilities, (b) perform its duties in
compliance with applicable laws and the applicable requirements of the agreements to
which the Owner is a party as set forth in Exhibit B and such additional contracts entered
into by the Company from time to time upon the Asset Manager's receipt of a copy of such
additional contract (the “Project Documents”), (c) exercise such care, skill and diligence
as a reasonably prudent business company of established reputation engaged in the energy
storage business would exercise in the conduct of its business and for the advancement or
protection of its own interests, (d) perform the duties in accordance with applicable energy
storage industry standards, taking into account the requirements to qualify for the ITC and
other tax benefits and (e) use sufficient and properly trained and skilled personnel. For the
avoidance of doubt, the foregoing sentence shall not obligate Manager to use optimum
practices or methods to the exclusion of all others, but rather to use a spectrum of
reasonable and prudent practices and methods that take into consideration the conditions
specific to the Owner and the Facilities.
Appointment of Representative: Asset Manager shall appoint a_ representative
(“Representative”) who will represent Asset Manager in connection with this Agreement
and will be responsible for receiving such approvals or instructions from the Owner that
may be required under the Standards from time to time. Owner will be entitled to rely on
the actions and statements of Representative for the purposes of this Agreement.
Governmental Approvals: Asset Manager will apply for, and shall obtain, maintain and
manage all governmental approvals, authorizations or permits (“Governmental
Authorizations”) (if any) that are necessary for Asset Manager to perform its obligations
under this Agreement. In the event that any such Governmental Authorization must be
issued in the name of the Owner, the Owner will provide reasonable assistance, to the
extent reasonably practicable, in obtaining such Governmental Authorizations, provided
that Asset Manager shall manage all such Governmental Authorizations issued in the name
of the Owner.
Facility Coordination. Asset Manager shall perform the daily maintenance and
monitoring of the Facilities, as more fully described in Exhibit B. All activities not
explicitly listed in Exhibit B shall be subject to the prior written approval of Owner.
Contractors: Asset Manager has the right to engage one or more contractors (which

maintaining all reasonable documentation that may be required as part of an ITC audit by

documenting the ITC Eligibility of the Facilities.
Insurance. Asset Manager shall procure and maintain the insurance specified in Exhibit
C during the Term.
Rental Terms with End Customers: As part of the Work, Asset Manager shall market
the Facilities and negotiate terms for rental agreements for the Facilities with End
Customers (“Rental Agreements”); provided, however, any such Rental Agreements shall
be executed in Owner’s name, and Asset Manager shall not have the authority to execute
any Rental Agreement or any other agreements or instruments on behalf of Owner and
shall not purport to agree to any terms on behalf of Owner or bind Owner pursuant to any
agreement without specific written authorization from the Owner..

3. COMPENSATION AND PAYMENT
a)
b)
Asset Management Fee: In exchange for the performance of the Work by Asset Manager,
Owner agrees to pay Asset Manager an amount calculated as a percentage of the aggregate
gross rental fees that Owner actually receives under all of the executed Rental Agreements with
End Users, subject to an aggregate maximum amount (the "Asset Management Fee"). The
specific percentage rate and aggregate maximum amount shall be mutually agreed upon by the
parties and set forth in an addendum to this Agreement within ninety (90) days of execution.
In the event the parties are unable to reach agreement on such terms within the specified
timeframe, the percentage rate and aggregate maximum amount shall be determined by binding
arbitration in accordance with the arbitration provisions set forth in this Agreement.
Notwithstanding the percentage-based fee structure, Asset Manager shall receive a minimum
management fee of Five Thousand Dollars ($5,000.00) per month per BESS unit for the
management of the battery array consisting of one hundred one (101) battery units under this
initial contract, which minimum fee shall apply regardless of whether the percentage terms
have been finalized. The monthly fee will commence 90 days after execution of this agreement
to be paid quarterly. For the avoidance of doubt, Owner shall have the sole right to receive the
rental fees from the End Users or any gross or net profits generated by Owner and Asset
Manager shall not seek to receive such rental fees directly and, if received, shall turn over any
such rentals fees to Owner within two (2) business days of receipt unless otherwise directed in
writing by the Owner.
Payment: Owner and Asset Manager agree that all payments to the Asset Manager shall be
paid by Owner on a quarterly basis in arrears in accordance with terms of this Agreement. All
payments will be in U.S. dollars. Owner shall provide Asset Manager with a report reflecting
to the total rental fees received by Owner under the Rental Agreements during the previous
calendar quarter within ten (10) business days of the end of each calendar quarter unless
otherwise directed in writing by the Owner.
Billing: Within twenty (20) business days following the end of each calendar quarter, Asset
Manager shall submit an invoice to Owner reflecting its calculation of the Asset Management
Fee due and payable for the previous quarter. Within fifteen (15) business days after receipt
of any such invoice, Owner will (i) by wire transfer pay to Asset Manager the sum specified in
such invoice, less any portion of such amount that Owner in good faith disputes, and (ii) with
respect to any disputed portion of such invoice, provide to Asset Manager a written statement
explaining in reasonable detail the basis for such dispute. The Parties shall attempt to resolve
any such disputed portion of any invoice in accordance with Section 9(g) hereof.

5. TERM AND TERMINATION
Term of Agreement: The initial term of this Agreement shall be five (5) years and the
Agreement shall automatically renew for successive 5-year terms, unless one of the Parties
gives a non-renewal notice to the other Party at least ninety (90) days before the last day
of the then-current term (the “Term”). Owner may terminate the Agreement for
convenience at any time by providing sixty (60) days’ written notice to Asset manager in
which case Owner shall pay Asset Manager a termination fee equal to 50% (fifty percent)
of the minimum monthly fee per BESS unit for the remaining term of the agreement.
Termination For Breach: This Agreement may be terminated at any time by either Party
if (i) the other Party becomes insolvent, or (ii) the other Party breaches any of its material
obligations under this Agreement and fails to cure such breach within thirty (30) days after
receiving notice of such breach.
Payments: Within fifteen (15) days following termination of this Agreement, Asset
Manager shall submit an invoice to Owner reflecting any Work performed by Asset
Manager prior to the termination of this Agreement for which an invoice had not previously
been delivered.
Transition Services. Upon expiration or termination of this Agreement, upon the Owner's
written request, Asset Manager shall continue to provide the Work hereunder and the
Owner shall continue to pay Asset Manager the Asset Management Fee, until such time as
the Owner finds a new replacement provider to provide the Work (provided, that Asset
Manager’s obligation to continue to provide the Work hereunder following such
termination shall not exceed one hundred eighty (180) days). Asset Manager shall perform
all transitional tasks reasonably necessary for the transition of the Work to Owner’s
replacement provider, including (i) delivering copies of all Facility documentation, (ii) to
the extent the Owner or the replacement provider requests, assign all subcontracts to such
replacement provider that are assignable, and (iii) use commercially reasonable efforts to
provide any other information that Owner or such replacement provider may reasonably
request (“Transition Services”).
Survival. Notwithstanding any provision of this Agreement to the contrary, the respective
rights and obligations of the Parties hereto under Sections 7 and 9 will survive the termination
of this Agreement.
