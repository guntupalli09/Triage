This Second Amended and Restated Dealer Manager Agreement (this “Agreement”) is entered into by and between PIMCO Asset-Based Lending Company LLC (together with its series, “PALCO” or the “Company”), and PIMCO Investments LLC (the “Dealer Manager”) and amends and restates in its entirety the prior Amended and Restated Dealer Manager Agreement between the Company and the Dealer Manager, dated September 29, 2025.

The Company is conducting a private placement offering (the “Offering”) in accordance with Rule 506(b) of Regulation D and Regulation S under the Securities Act of 1933, as amended (the “Securities Act”), of the Company’s limited liability company interests (the “Shares”), which may consist of Anchor I Shares, Anchor I-B Shares, Anchor II Shares, Anchor II-B Shares, Anchor III Shares, Standard A Shares and Standard B Shares and any other classes of Shares added by the Company’s Board of Directors (the “Board”) from time to time (each, a “Class”).

Under the terms of the Offering, as set forth in the Company’s confidential private placement memorandum related to the Offering (including any supplements and amendments thereto, all financial statements, appendices and all other documents which are part thereof, the “Memorandum”), Shares will be issued and sold on a continuous monthly basis at a purchase price equal to the Company’s net asset value (“NAV”) per Share as set forth in the Memorandum. In connection with the Offering, the minimum initial subscription amount by any one person shall be as set forth in the Memorandum (except as otherwise accepted by the Dealer Manager pursuant to its discretion to accept lesser amounts).

The differences between the Classes of Shares and the eligibility requirements for each Class are described in detail in the Memorandum. The Shares are to be offered and sold as described in the Memorandum. Except as otherwise agreed by the Company and the Dealer Manager, Shares are to be sold through the Dealer Manager, as the dealer manager, and the broker-dealers (each a “Dealer” and collectively, the “Dealers”) with whom the Dealer Manager has entered into or will enter into a selected dealer agreement related to the distribution of Shares substantially in the form attached to this Agreement as Exhibit B or such other form as approved by the Company (each, a “Selected Dealer Agreement”). For shareholders who have not “opted out” of the Company’s distribution reinvestment plan (the “DRIP”), the cash distributions attributable to the Class of Shares that each shareholder owns will be automatically reinvested in additional Shares of the same Class. The DRIP Shares are to be issued to shareholders of the Company at a purchase price equal to the applicable NAV per Share for such Shares at the time the distribution is payable.

Terms not defined herein shall have the same meaning as in the Memorandum. Now, therefore, the Company hereby agrees with the Dealer Manager as follows:
