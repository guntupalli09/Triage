the Financial Advisor, for the period beginning on the date hereof and ending 30 days from the date
hereof; (the “Engagement Period”). During the Enagagement Period, The Financial Advisor will assist
the Company in a contemplated debt offering. It is understood that the Financial Advisor is acting as
an advisor to the private debt transaction and will not be is not obligated to undertake any duties, due

information or to render any analytical support or inputs or to spend any minimum amount of time in
providing any services pursuant to this Agreement. The Financial Advisor hereby agrees to assist in
facilitating the Transaction provided, however, that the Financial Advisor’s duties and responsibilities
under this Agreement are expressly limited to exclude any activity or function that is traditionally
performed by a “broker” as such term is defined in Section 3(a)(4) of the Securities Exchange Act of
1934. The Company is encouraged to perform its own due diligence and analyses concerning the
Transaction. The decision to consummate a Transaction as well as the aggregate consideration amount
and structure shall be determined by the Company in its sole and absolute discretion.
2. Advisory Compensation. If the contemplated Transaction is consummated
during the Engagement Period, the Company will pay to Financial Advisor a one-time advisory fee of
$10,000 at closing.
