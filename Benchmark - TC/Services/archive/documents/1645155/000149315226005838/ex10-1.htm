EXCHANGE LICENSING AGREEMENT
This Agreement is entered into as of this second day of February 2026 (the “Effective Date”),
by and between:
e Torch LLC, a Georgia limited liability company, with its principal business address at
1050 Crown Pointe Parkway, Suite 500, Atlanta, GA 30338 (“Torch” or “Licensor”): and
e Forge Atlanta Asset Management, LLC, a Georgia limited liability company, or its
designated Special Purpose Vehicle (“FAAM/SPV” or “Licensee”).
Each may be referred to as a “Party” and collectively as the “Parties.”
RECITALS
WHEREAS, Torch operates a proprietary technology exchange designed to support the regulated
tokenization of real-world assets using blockchain infrastructure, including deployment on the
Ethereum blockchain and compliance with ERC-3643 security token standards (the “Platform”),
and;
WHEREAS, FAAM/SPV is the developer and asset manager of the Forge Atlanta Development
Project (the “Project”), and seeks to tokenize a portion of its economic interests in the Project,
and;
WHEREAS, the grant of the license provides Torch with significant economic benefit, and;
WHEREAS, FAAM/SPYV desires to grant Torch a limited, conditional license to issue ERC-3643
compliant Real-World Asset (RWA) tokens through the Platform, subject at all times to full
regulatory compliance, and;
WHEREAS, FAAM/SPV acknowledges that it shall act as the issuer of the RWA tokens and
shall bear sole responsibility for SEC registration, exemption, and disclosure obligations, and;
NOW, THEREFORE, the Parties agree as follows:
I. DEFINITIONS
A. “RWA Tokens” — ERC-3643 compliant blockchain-based digital securities
representing fractionalized economic interests in the Project.
B. “Primary Market” — Torch’s issuance infrastructure for offering tokens to
registered and verified (accredited) investors.
c. “Secondary Market” — Torch’s peer-to-peer trading environment for compliant
resale and purchase of circulating tokens.
D. “Regulatory Approval” — All required registrations, qualifications, exemptions, or
effectiveness determinations from the SEC or applicable state/foreign authorities.

GRANT OF LICENSE
FAAM/SPV grants Torch a non-exclusive, non-transferable, revocable, and conditional
license to:
A. issue ERC-3643 compliant RWA tokens representing fractionalized economic
interests in the Project;
B. offer such tokens exclusively through Torch’s Primary Market to registered and
verified (accredited) investors;
Cc. enable compliant peer-to-peer resale and purchase of circulating tokens via
Torch’s Secondary Market; and
D. utilize Torch’s compliance framework, custody integrations, and transaction
tracking systems.
E. Torch may promote the FAAM/SPV RWA tokens within its own framework,
publications and press releases with prior approval by FAAM/SPV.
F. Torch agrees to pay FAAM/SPV 3% of the total tokenization in cash or cash
equivalents within twelve months of the execution of this agreement for the rights to list
the RWA tokens.
