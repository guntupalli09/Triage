Each Party represents and warrants that:

(a)
The discount or Rebate represents a bona fide discount and is not offered to induce or reward referrals or purchases of any items or services reimbursable under a Federal health care program;

(b)
Vendor shall disclose the discount or Rebate on the applicable invoice(s) or payment remittance in a manner sufficient to permit HMS and Facilities to meet their reporting obligations under the Safe Harbor;
