Role Responsibilities
As Chief Revenue Officer, you will be responsible for:
+ Deepening and expanding the amount of NusaTrip’s flight and inventory channel partners,
specifically increasing the number and variety of inventory channel partners such as airlines and
flight and hotel inventory suppliers in China and internationally to obtain more competitive
inventory costing.
+ Deepening and expanding NusaTrip’s customer base in China and internationally and owning
end-to-end accountability for NusaTrip's B2B sales performance, specifically increasing the
number and variety of customers such as OTAs and other B2B clients to obtain more profitable
margins.
- Identifying, negotiating, and onboarding new China and international-based OTA partners
including platforms such as Ctrip, Qunar, Fliggy, and equivalent-scale distributors.
- Building and leading a China regional sales management team of 1-2 members within 6 months
of your start date.
Sales Growth Plan and KPI Targets
Your performance will be assessed against the following agreed targets. Specific customer names and
individual account plans will be documented and agreed with you within the first 30 days.

the first 30 days. NusaTrip will provide reasonable support for team establishment costs subject to prior
written approval.

Confidentiality and Exclusivit
This role is a full-time, exclusive engagement. During your employment with NusaTrip, you agree not to
undertake any concurrent employment, consultancy, or directorship with any competing online travel
agency, airline distribution platform, or B2B travel supplier without prior written consent from NusatTrip’s
CEO.
You agree to maintain strict confidentiality in respect of NusaTrip's airline API connections, partner
identities, pricing, commercial terms, and customer data, both during and after your employment.
Acceptance
This offer is open for acceptance for 5 business days from the date of this letter. To accept, please sign
and return this letter to the CEO. Commencement of work prior to signing constitutes acceptance of all
terms set out herein.
