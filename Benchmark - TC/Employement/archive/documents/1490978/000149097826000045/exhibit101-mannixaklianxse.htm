This Transition, Separation, and Release of Claims Agreement (the “Agreement”) is entered into by and between Schrödinger, Inc. (the “Company”) and Mannix Aklian (“Executive”) (together, the “Parties”).
WHEREAS, the Company and Executive are parties to the Employment Agreement dated as of May 9, 2025 (the “Employment Agreement”);
