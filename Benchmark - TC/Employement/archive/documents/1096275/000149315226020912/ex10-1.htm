EXECUTIVE EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT
This Executive Employment Agreement (this “Agreement”) is entered into as of January 1, 2026, by
and between Jennifer Kartychak (“Executive”), an individual, and Worksport USA Operations
Corporation, a Colorado corporation (“Worksport”).
WHEREAS, Worksport desires to employ Executive on a full-time basis and Executive desires to be
so employed, subject to the terms and conditions set forth in this Agreement; and
WHEREAS, Worksport and Executive recognize that while employment is at-will and may be
terminated by either party as set forth herein, it is a shared intent for both parties to enter into this Agreement
with a good faith expectation of a productive, collaborative, and long-term employment relationship
NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the foregoing recitals, the mutual covenants and conditions
herein, and other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which is hereby
acknowledged, Worksport and Executive agree as follows:
1. Employment; Duties _and_ Responsibilities. Worksport will employ Executive as its Vice
President (VP) of Finance, reporting to Worksport’s Chief Executive Officer (“CEO”), and Executive
accepts employment to serve in such capacity, all upon the terms and conditions set forth in this Agreement.
Executive shall have such duties and responsibilities as are consistent with Executive’s position as VP of
Finance of Worksport, subject to the specific direction of Worksport’s Board of Directors (“Board”)
Executive shall also have such other duties, powers, and authority as are commensurate with her position
as VP of Finance and such other duties and responsibilities that are commensurate with her position as
specifically delegated to her from time to time by the CEO and/or the Board. It is anticipated that upon
successful performance of her duties for the first four (4) months as VP of Finance, Executive shall be
promoted to Chief Finance Officer (“CFO”) of Worksport. Worksport reserves the right, in its sole
discretion, to change or modify Executive's position, title and duties during the term of this Agreement,
subject to Section 8(g)(2)(i), provided that any such changes to Executive’s reporting line or duties will be
communicated to Executive in writing with at least 30 days’ notice. Executive shall at all times comply
with all applicable federal, state, and local laws and regulations, including but not limited to those of the
SEC, NASDAQ, FTC, CPSC, OSHA, and any other regulatory authorities relevant to Worksport’s business
as a publicly traded company and manufacturer in the automotive and energy sectors.
2. Term. Executive will commence her employment as VP of Finance of Worksport under the
terms of this Agreement starting on January 1, 2026 (‘Commencement Date”). Executive will be
employed under this Agreement on an at-will basis, until Executive’s employment is terminated
pursuant to Section 8 herein.
3. Compliance_with Worksport Policies. Executive shall be subject to the bylaws, policies,
practices, procedures and rules of Worksport, including those policies and procedures set forth in
Worksport’s Code of Conduct and Ethics. Executive’s violation of the terms of such documents shall be
considered a breach of the terms of this Agreement and may result in immediate termination of employment
without further obligation by Worksport.
4. Location. The duties to be performed by Executive hereunder shall be performed remotely at
Executive’s place of residence during hours consistent with the Eastern Time Zone. Relocation shall not be
required and continued employment with Worksport shall not be contingent on relocation.

$220,000 per year. The Base Salary will be payable in accordance with the payroll practices of Worksport,
which is currently bi-weekly. Executive’s Base Salary will be reviewed at least annually in accordance with
