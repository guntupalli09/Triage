EXECUTIVE EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT
This Executive Employment Agreement (the “Agreement”) is entered into as of
January 26, 2026 (the “Effective Date”) by and between Bruce Czachor (“Executive”), and
Stardust Power Inc. (the “Company”). The Company and Executive are each sometimes referred
to as a “Party,” and collectively as the “Parties.”
WHEREAS, the Company wishes to employ Executive, and Executive wishes to accept
employment with the Company, as the General Counsel, Chief Compliance Officer and Secretary
of the Company, pursuant to the terms and conditions set forth in this Agreement.
NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual promises and covenants contained
herein, it is hereby agreed by and between the Parties hereto as follows:

For purposes of the Agreement, the following terms are defined as follows:
1.1. “Board” means the Board of Directors of the Company.
1.2. “Cause” means any of the following: (A) the willful failure by Executive to substantially
perform Executive’s duties under this Agreement (other than as a result of illness, Disability,
or other incapacity);; (B) willful breach of fiduciary duty involving personal benefit; (C) willful
breach by Executive of any of the restrictive covenants set forth in Articles V, VI or VII of this
Agreement; (D) conviction or guilty plea or plea of nolo contendere of Executive for any
misdemeanor involving dishonesty or moral turpitude or any felony; or (E) any other willful
conduct that is demonstrably and materially injurious to the Company, its business, or its

regular payroll dates of the Company (but no less often than monthly), subject to increase in the

eligible to receive an annual performance bonus (the “Annual Bonus’) (i) targeted at seventy-
five percent (75%) of Base Salary (the “Target Bonus”) or such other amount as determined
in the sole discretion of the Board or a committee of the Board, (i1) with a maximum Annual
Bonus opportunity equal to two hundred percent (200%) of the Target Bonus, (ii1) on such terms
and conditions determined by the Board or a committee of the Board, and (iv) payable at
Company’s sole election in cash or shares of the Company’s common stock, or some
combination of both, provided, however, that any shares of stock issued as part of an Annual
Bonus shall vest immediately and not be subject to any restrictions on transfer (other than

determined in the discretion of the Board or a committee of the Board and will be (i) subject to
