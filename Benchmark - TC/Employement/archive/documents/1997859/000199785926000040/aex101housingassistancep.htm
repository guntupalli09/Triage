WwEB
Housing Assistance Policy
Effective Date
4/1/2026
Purpose
The purpose of this policy is to facilitate relocation of the Chief Executive Officer's (CEO) primary
residence from Korea to the United States.
This support is intended to ensure early stabilization of the executive's living environment and provide a
residence that meets the required standards for personal safety and security, thereby enabling the
focused execution of business objectives.
Eligibility
e This benefit is exclusively available to the Chief Executive Officer (CEO) of WEBTOON
Entertainment Inc.
e The CEO must be actively employed at the time of payment to be eligible for this benefit.
Benefit Details
e Annual Support Amount: The fixed annual support amount is $250,350 in cash.
e Duration: Housing support will be provided for a period of two years to ensure stable settlement
following the relocation. Any extension beyond the initial two-year period will be reviewed
considering the schedule for the disposal of the executive's residence in Korea and overall
business requirements.
e Payment Method: It will be provided as a fixed cash allowance, paid on a monthly basis, and is
not subject to reimbursement of actual expenses.
Other Considerations
e Calculation Basis: The amount is determined based on the Mercer Mobility criteria for global
leadership assignments (calculated at an annual base of $166,900).
e To offset the individual income tax impact on the executive, the support amount has been
determined on a grossed-up basis, taking into account applicable income taxes.
Disclaimer
WEBTOON reserves the right to interpret, modify, or withdraw from this plan at any time, subject to
applicable legal requirements.
(End)
