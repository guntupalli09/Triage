Job Description
Chief Financial Officer (CFO) — Cycurion, Inc.
Position Summary
Cycurion, Inc. is seeking a strategic, operationally rigorous Chief Financial Officer to serve as the senior
finance leader for a publicly traded cybersecurity and technology company listed on Nasdaq under the
symbol CYCU. The CFO reports directly to the Chief Executive Officer and partners closely with the Board
of Directors and Audit Committee to lead financial strategy, public company reporting, capital planning,
internal controls, treasury, tax, investor support, and scalable finance operations. The CFO will help
accelerate Cycurion’s path to profitable growth while strengthening the financial discipline, compliance
infrastructure, and performance visibility required of a Nasdaq-listed company operating in
cybersecurity, Al-driven solutions, and government and enterprise markets.
Key Responsibilities
Lead all finance and accounting functions, including controllership, SEC reporting support,
FP&A, treasury, tax, audit coordination, cash management, and financial operations.
Serve as a principal advisor to the CEO on financial strategy, capital allocation, liquidity planning,
profitability improvement, pricing discipline, and growth investments.
Oversee timely and accurate public company reporting, including support for quarterly and
annual filings, earnings materials, disclosure controls, and readiness for Nasdaq and SEC
compliance requirements.
Strengthen internal controls over financial reporting, accounting policies, close processes, and
governance practices to support a high-confidence reporting environment.
Develop and institutionalize a forward-looking planning cadence that includes annual operating
plans, rolling forecasts, scenario analysis, backlog conversion visibility, and margin performance
tracking.
Partner with business leaders to improve revenue quality, gross margin, operating expense
discipline, working capital performance, and cash conversion.
Support Board and Audit Committee materials with clear, decision-ready financial analyses, key
risk indicators, and performance dashboards.
Lead treasury and capital structure planning, including cash forecasting, financing readiness,
investor-facing support materials, and evaluation of strategic capital alternatives.
Provide financial leadership for acquisitions, integrations, diligence, purchase accounting
coordination, and synergy tracking.
Build a scalable finance organization with clear accountability, process discipline, strong systems
utilization, and high-quality talent development.
Partner cross-functionally with legal, operations, sales, human resources, and technology
leaders to align financial controls and planning with business execution.
Ensure compliance with applicable tax, audit, regulatory, and corporate governance
requirements.
Candidate Profile
Proven experience as a CFO, Deputy CFO, VP Finance, or equivalent senior finance executive in a
public company or a company preparing for or operating under public company standards.
Deep knowledge of SEC reporting, public company controls, external audit coordination, GAAP,
budgeting, forecasting, and Board reporting.
Experience in technology, cybersecurity, SaaS, government contracting, or complex services
environments is strongly preferred.
