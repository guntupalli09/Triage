TILLY’S, INC. FOURTH AMENDED AND RESTATED 2012 EQUITY AND INCENTIVE AWARD PLAN
Article 1.

PURPOSE
The purpose of the Tilly’s, Inc. Fourth Amended and Restated 2012 Equity and Incentive Award Plan (as it may be amended or restated from time to time, the “Plan”) is to promote the success and enhance the value of Tilly’s, Inc. (the “Company”) by linking the individual interests of the members of the Board, Employees, and Consultants to those of Company stockholders and by providing such individuals with an incentive for outstanding performance to generate superior returns to Company stockholders. The Plan is further intended to provide flexibility to the Company in its ability to motivate, attract, and retain the services of members of the Board, Employees, and Consultants upon whose judgment, interest, and special effort the successful conduct of the Company’s operation is largely dependent. The Plan amends and restates in its entirety the Tilly’s, Inc. Third Amended and Restated 2012 Equity and Incentive Award Plan (the “Original Plan”).
