Title: Chief Financial Officer
Congratulations and welcome to Massimo Group. I am very pleased that you have
chosen to accept our offer of employment and know that this is the beginning of a mutually
beneficial association.
We will do our best to help you become a productive member of our staff through
training. In the next few weeks, you will meet coworkers, supervisors and managers. Feel
free to let them know what you need to accomplish your new responsibilities.
GENERAL PURPOSE
The general purpose of this agreement is to establish and maintain harmonious
relationships, provide a procedure for prompt and equitable disposition of grievances, hours
of work, wages, and employee benefits in order to achieve an efficient and productive
environment and the highest possible level of employee performance.
Position and Duties
The Company hereby employs Executive as Chief Financial Officer (CFO) of the
Company, reporting directly to the Chief Executive Officer.
Executive shall perform the duties customarily associated with the position of Chief
Financial Officer, including oversight of accounting, financial reporting, budgeting, internal
controls, and other financial functions as reasonably assigned.
Effective Date
Executive’s formal employment and appointment as Chief Financial Officer shall be
effective as of March 2, 2026.

Any assistance provided by Executive to the Company prior to the Effective Date was
on an informal, transitional basis only and does not constitute employment as an officer of

Compensation
Beginning on the Effective Date, Executive shall receive:
- Base Salary: USD $100,000 per year, payable in accordance with the Company’s
regular payroll practices.
- Bonus Eligibility: Executive may be eligible for discretionary bonuses as determined
by the Company from time to time.
- Benefits: Participation in Company benefit plans, subject to plan terms and
availability.
Employment Status
Executive’s employment with the Company is at-will, meaning either the Company or
Executive may terminate the employment relationship at any time, with or without cause or
notice, subject to applicable law.
Confidentiality
Executive agrees to maintain the confidentiality of all non-public, proprietary, and
confidential information of the Company, both during and after employment.
Compliance with Policies
Executive agrees to comply with all Company policies, including but not limited to
codes of conduct, insider trading policies, and confidentiality requirements.
No Board Appointment
Executive acknowledges and agrees that this Agreement does not provide for
appointment to the Company’s Board of Directors.
