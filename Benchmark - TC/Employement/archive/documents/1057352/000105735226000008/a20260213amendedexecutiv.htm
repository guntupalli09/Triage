I. PURPOSE
The purpose of this CoStar Group, Inc. Amended Executive Severance Plan (the “Plan”) is to
encourage employees of CoStar Group, Inc. (together with any successor, the “Company”) and its
subsidiaries to remain in the employ of the Employer by providing, among other things, severance
protections to such employees in the event their employment is terminated under the circumstances
described in this Plan.
