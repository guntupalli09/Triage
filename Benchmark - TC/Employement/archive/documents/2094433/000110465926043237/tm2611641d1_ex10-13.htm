Pursuant to this Performance Stock Unit Award Grant Notice (this “Notice of Grant”), you (the “Participant”) are hereby granted a Performance Stock Unit Award (the “Award”) by Suncrete, Inc., a Delaware corporation (the “Company”) under the Suncrete, Inc. 2026 Omnibus Incentive Plan (the “Plan”), as set forth below.
