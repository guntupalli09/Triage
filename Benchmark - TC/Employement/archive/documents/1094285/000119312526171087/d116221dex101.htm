ARTICLE 1.

PURPOSE

The purpose of the Amended and Restated Teledyne Technologies Incorporated 2014 Incentive Award Plan, as further amended and restated herein (the “Plan”) is to promote the success and enhance the value of Teledyne Technologies Incorporated by linking the personal interests of the members of the Board, Employees, and Consultants to those of Company stockholders and by providing such individuals with an incentive for outstanding performance to generate superior returns to Company stockholders. The Plan is further intended to provide flexibility to the Company in its ability to motivate, attract, and retain the services of members of the Board, Employees, and Consultants upon whose judgment, interest, and special effort the successful conduct of the Company’s operation is largely dependent. The Plan amends and restates in its entirety the Amended and Restated Teledyne Technologies Incorporated 2014 Incentive Award Plan (the “First Amended Plan”).
