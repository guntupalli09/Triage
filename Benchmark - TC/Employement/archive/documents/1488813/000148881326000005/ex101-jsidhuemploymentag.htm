cliff vest 100% after three years resulting in a final payout that may be higher or lower than the
target number of performance units, based on performance-based criteria approved by the
LD&CC. LTI awards are typically granted in April of the calendar year following the end of the
respective performance year (i.e., April 2027 for performance year 2026). LTI awards are not
earned until they vest and remain subject to all policies and/or plan provisions under which they
were awarded.
