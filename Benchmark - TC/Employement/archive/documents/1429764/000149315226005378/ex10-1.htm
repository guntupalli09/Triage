SEPARATION AGREEMENT AND GENERAL RELEASE

 

This Separation Agreement and General Release (“Agreement”) is by and between Blink Charging Company (“Employer”) and Aviv Hillo (“Employee”), who are collectively referred to herein as the “Parties” and each as a “Party.”

 

WHEREAS, Employer and Employee entered into a General Counsel & EVP of M&A Employment Agreement, which was executed on or around April 25, 2025 (“Employment Agreement”) and is attached hereto as “Exhibit A”);

 

WHEREAS, pursuant to Section 27 of the Employment Agreement, Employer and Employee have mutually agreed that the Employment Agreement and Employee’s employment with Employer terminated effective January 31, 2026, and Employer has agreed to offer Employee certain separation benefits on the terms described herein; and

 

WHEREAS, the Parties now desire and agree to forever sever their employment relationship and to fully and finally resolve any and all existing or potential issues, claims, causes of action, grievances and disputes without any admission of liability or finding or admission that any of Employee’s rights, under any statute, claim or otherwise, were in any way violated.

 

IN CONSIDERATION OF the mutual promises contained herein, and other good and valuable consideration as hereinafter recited, the receipt and adequacy of which is hereby acknowledged, the Parties, intending to be legally bound, agree as follows:

 

1. Recitals. The foregoing recitals are hereby made a part of this Agreement.

 

2. Separation Date. The Employment Agreement and Employee’s employment with Employer terminated effective January 31, 2026 (“Separation Date”). Employee is not authorized to represent Employer after the Separation Date. The Parties acknowledge and agree that Employee’s separation from employment is the result of the Parties’ mutual and voluntary decision. The Parties further acknowledge and agree that this termination of the Employment Agreement and Employee’s employment with Employer does not constitute a Termination for Good Reason, a Termination for Cause or a Termination Without Cause (as each such term is defined in the Employment Agreement) and that all notice requirements, if any, have been expressly waived by the Parties. Employee further acknowledges, agrees and represents that Employee hereby resigns from all positions Employee holds as an officer or member of the Board of Directors (and any committee thereof) of Employer (“Board”) or any of its affiliates effective the Separation Date. Employee represents that Employee’s resignation from the Board is not the result of any disagreement with the Company on any matter relating to the Company’s operations, policies, or practices. Employee agrees to promptly notify the Company if Employee becomes aware that this representation was not accurate when made or becomes inaccurate prior to the Company’s filing of any Current Report on Form 8-K (“8-K”) with the Securities and Exchange Commission (the “SEC”) reporting the resignation. Employee shall have the right to review a draft of the 8-K that references this Agreement or Employee’s separation solely for the limited purpose of identifying factual inaccuracies and information that is personal and confidential to Employee. Employee’s review shall be limited to one round of comments and Employee shall deliver any written comments to Employer within twenty-four (24) hours after receipt of the draft. Employer will consider in good faith any comments submitted by Employee, but Employer shall have the sole and exclusive authority to determine the final content of any such filing and may file such Form 8-K or other public disclosure without Employee’s consent if required by applicable law, rule, or stock exchange requirement or if Employee fails to provide comments within the twenty-four (24) hour review period. The Parties acknowledge that Employer may redact competitively sensitive information or personal data from any exhibit to the extent permitted under applicable SEC rules and that any filing made by Employer will be consistent with Employer’s disclosure obligations and with advice of its legal counsel.

 

 

 

 

3. Earned Compensation. Without regard to Employee’s acceptance of this Agreement, Employee will be paid (a) through the Separation Date, at Employee’s regular wage; and (b) for accrued, unused paid time off to which Employee is entitled pursuant to Employer policy (through the Separation Date) of 240 hours, both such amounts subject to applicable deductions and withholdings. Employee acknowledges and declares that, provided that Employer delivers to Employee the payments described in this Section 2, Employee will have been fully compensated for all work performed on Employer’s behalf through the Separation Date, and that Employee is not owed any compensation, wages, salary, severance, commissions, payments, bonus, longevity bonus, equity interest, remuneration or income from Employer or any Releasee (as defined herein) of any kind, except as provided in this Agreement.

 

4. Consideration. If Employee has neither revoked this Agreement pursuant to Section 11 nor breached any of its provisions, Employer shall:

 

a. Pay to Employee a total sum of $552,610, less applicable deductions and withholdings. This payment shall be made in a single lump sum at or before the end of Employer’s next, full payroll cycle following the Effective Date (as defined in Section 11);

 

b. Pay on behalf of Employee, Employee’s cost of health insurance continuation coverage pursuant to the Consolidated Omnibus Budget Reconciliation Act (“COBRA”) at Employee’s level of health insurance benefits in effect as of the Separation Date through January 31, 2027, provided Employee timely elects continuation coverage. Employee acknowledges and agrees that should Employee fail to timely elect such coverage, Employer is not obligated to provide compensation or otherwise make payments related to health insurance on behalf of Employee in lieu thereof;

 

c. Provide a new grant of fully vested restricted stock units under the Employer’s equity incentive plan, the number of which shall be determined by dividing $650,000 by the closing price of a share of Blink Charging Co. common stock (ticker BLNK) on the last trading day before the Board approves this grant;

 

d. Provide full vesting of all other restricted stock units (RSUs), restricted stock awards (RSAs), stock options and warrants under the Employer’s equity incentive plan held by the Employee; and

 

e. Permit Employee to retain ownership of, and not seek reimbursement for, the Employer-owned laptop (“Laptop”) provided to Employee by Employer during employment. Employee acknowledges and agrees that Employer is not responsible to Employee, or any governing body, for any taxes related to Employee’s ownership of the Laptop or the transfer of such ownership. Accordingly, Employee agrees to pay all federal, state, and local taxes resulting from Employee’s ownership of the Laptop. For such purposes, Employee acknowledges and agrees the fair market value of the Laptop is Five Hundred Dollars ($500).

 

Withholding for taxes due under Sections 4(c) and 4(d) shall be accomplished by net settlement of the restricted stock units, with the Employer holding back and not issuing the number of shares of BLNK stock necessary to cover the minimum required tax withholding amounts.

 

The adequacy of these “Separation Benefits” is hereby acknowledged. Employee agrees that Employee is not entitled to the Separation Benefits absent Employee’s execution and non-revocation of this Agreement.

2.1.Engagement. The Company wishes to continue employing the Executive as its General Counsel and EVP of M&A (“Executive”), a position that Executive hereby accepts.
