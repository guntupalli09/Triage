RESOLUTION OF THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF
HEALTHCARE INTEGRATED TECHNOLOGIES, INC.
April 15, 2025
The undersigned Board of Directors (the “Board”) of Healthcare Integrated Technologies,
Inc., a Nevada corporation (the “Company”), hereby adopt the following resolution in a remote
meeting held for such purposes on April 15, 2025.
WHEREAS, the Chairman motioned for FKP Advisors LLC to be appointed to the Board
of Directors of the Company for a three (3) year term and that FKP Advisors LLC compensation
as a member of the Company’s Board shall include an initial stock grant and a three (3) year
restricted stock award totaling 400,000 shares of the Company’s common stock (the “Motion”).
FKP Advisors LLC board seat will be on a rotational basis with Larry Kloess III serving in the first
year followed by Jim Fitzgerald in the second year and Ben Pope in the third year. The annual stock
grant will be divided by three (“3”) each year and awarded to each individual.
NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED, that, upon the Motion by the Chairman, the
Board voted three (4) directors for approval of the Motion. The Motion hereby passes by a vote of
the majority of the Board.
FURTHER RESOLVED, that FKP Advisors LLC is hereby appointed as a member of
the Company’s Board for a term of three (3) years to be effective on the date of this Consent. Both
the Company and FKP Advisors LLC shall have the right to cancel the remaining term of FDP
Advisors’ Board appointment on each yearly anniversary date by providing written notice to the
other party at least thirty (30) days prior to the anniversary date the cancelation is to
become effective. As compensation for participation on the Board, the Company shall grant FKP
Advisors LLC a restricted stock award of 400,000 shares of the Company’s common stock, to
become vested in accordance with the following vesting schedule:
133,333 Shares April 15, 2026
133,333 Shares April 15, 2027
133,334 Shares April 15, 2028
IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the undersigned have executed and delivered this written
consent as of the date first indicated above.
